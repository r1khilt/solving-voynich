# From position crossover to a reusable causal intermediate

Date: 2026-09-22. **Status: theory and experiment design only.** No model was trained, scored, or intervened on for this memo. This is an adaptive proposal after PATH-0004; its discovery panel cannot serve as a fresh confirmation set. The manuscript's decipherment remains the long-term objective.

## What is known, and what the architecture already predicts

The registered PATH-0004 verdict is **inconclusive**: the confirmation panel had only 10/24 both-clean-correct composed queries against a required 12/24, so no confirmation intervention ran. In its exposed discovery panel (14 eligible queries from six dependent lexical bundles), first-table-key residual transplants produced donor full answers in 12/14 at block 8, 5/14 at block 16, and 0/14 at block 24; the downstream-suffix transplants produced 1/14, 8/14, and 10/14. Their union produced 13/14, 14/14, and 10/14. Random suffix edits produced 0/14 throughout; unrelated copying remained 12/12. The suffix includes the second table **and later question text**. A late whole-prompt transplant produced 14/14, whereas an all-earlier transplant produced 10/14, so the final query position may also matter. These are observed intervention outcomes, not identified information transfers or a confirmed composed-lookup mechanism. [PATH-0004 registration](../../experiments/PATH-0004.md); [result and audit](../../experiments/PATH-0004-results.md).

The stronger prior PATH-0002 result is for **direct, supplied English key–value lookup**, not composition: at block 24 value-field patches redirected 45/46 eligible answers versus 0/46 norm-matched random; at block 32 the final query site redirected 41/46 while the value field redirected 1/46. That establishes a position-dependent *effective control point* for this task, not a unique stored variable. [PATH-0002 result](../../experiments/PATH-0002-results.md).

A causal transformer's earlier token can affect later tokens through attention, and an early residual transplant changes **all** downstream attention and MLP computation. An early-to-late change in effective patching site is therefore an architectural expectation. The interesting empirical questions are whether the transported content is a compositional **intermediate key**, whether a specific read from it is necessary or sufficient, and whether that explanation predicts new counterfactual outputs when the downstream lookup changes. [Elhage et al.'s transformer-circuit framework](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html) supplies the QK addressing / OV transport distinction; it does not make our crossover a special workspace. [Zhang and Nanda](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042) show that activation-patching conclusions can change with corruption and metric choices. [Geiger et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709) formalize the stronger goal as a high-level causal abstraction faithful to low-level interventions.

## The decisive new question: does the patch carry a *key* or inject an *answer*?

Use a **factorial two-table task** with first table \(F_i\) and independently varied second table \(G_j\). For one name \(n\), let \(F_0(n)=k_0\), \(F_1(n)=k_1\); choose counterbalanced output mappings \(G_0(k_0)=a\), \(G_0(k_1)=b\), \(G_1(k_0)=c\), \(G_1(k_1)=d\), with four distinct output tokens where tokenizer constraints permit. The same upstream contrast \(F_0\to F_1\) should make the answer \(b\) under \(G_0\) but \(d\) under \(G_1\). The *value of this test* is that the upstream edit cannot succeed by delivering one fixed donor answer for both downstream tables. A fresh \(G_2\) with unseen outputs is a stronger transfer test.

Clean model competence comes first. Qualify the exact prompt family on disjoint lexical bundles, orientations and query names before selecting a layer or mediator. If the cached 8B model again fails a preregistered clean-composition gate, stop interpretability scoring and change the task format or use a purposely trained compositional teacher whose held-out keys, mappings and templates genuinely generalize. The teacher's training distribution and architecture then become part of the scientific claim. A teacher that memorizes a finite table or has evaluation keys seen during training is not a positive control for abstraction. Do not lower a gate after inspecting intervention outcomes; use a separate calibration panel for task design, then freeze new discovery and confirmation panels. This addresses the actual PATH-0004 failure rather than simply spending more compute on a fragile task.

Counterbalance key and answer token length, source/donor order, table order, query position, formatting and output frequency; record tokenizer IDs and exact prompt lengths. Where equal tokenization is impossible, make the mismatch explicit and include matched controls. Hold out **lexical bundles**, not multiple reversals of the same bundle, for confirmation and uncertainty. Report all attempted records as well as the both-clean-correct subset: conditioning only on successes estimates a narrower behavior and may bias the sample. Never reuse PATH-0003/0004 bundles as fresh evidence.

### A precise behavioral estimand

For source prompt \(x_{0j}\), upstream post-block site \(U\) and source/donor residual states \(h_U(x_{0j})\), \(h_U(x_{1j})\), define \(P_U(x_{0j};x_{1j})\) as the original model run on **source tokens** after replacing only \(h_U(x_{0j})\) with \(h_U(x_{1j})\) and recomputing every later block. Let \(s_j(r)=\log p_r(G_j(k_1))-\log p_r(G_j(k_0))\) at the answer position (or the equivalent two-token logit margin). The paired effect is

\[
\tau_j=s_j(P_U(x_{0j};x_{1j}))-s_j(x_{0j}),\qquad j\in\{0,1,2\}.
\]

This is a **model-internal intervention effect**, not a natural language causal effect or information-theoretic proof. Predeclare a full-answer correctness metric beside the continuous first-token margin; the two can disagree because of formatting. An optional normalized transport ratio divides \(\tau_j\) by the clean donor–source margin only when the denominator exceeds a prespecified floor; otherwise report the unnormalized margin and exclude the unstable ratio, not the record. A functional-key result requires the patch to select the output appropriate to **each independently set \(G_j\)**, with failures and negative controls reported by bundle. It still does not identify a unique latent direction.

## From a broad position patch to a tested path

Only after that functional test passes should discovery localize a candidate mediator. In a value-patched source run \(R_P\), capture a downstream head's **attention write** at the intended recipient position and block, \(M_P\). Capture \(M_0\) from the unpatched source run \(R_0\) with the same source input tokens. These captures differ because of the upstream intervention; avoid substituting a write from a separately changed donor prompt, which may import other answer cues. For a prespecified score \(s\), test both:

\[
N_M=s(R_P)-s(R_P[M\leftarrow M_0]),\qquad
S_M=s(R_0[M\leftarrow M_P])-s(R_0).
\]

\(N_M\) is the effect of reverting one candidate write within the upstream-patched computation; \(S_M\) is the effect of injecting that write into the unpatched source computation. These are controlled hybrid-run effects, **not** an automatically identified natural indirect effect. Both must be measured because parallel routes and downstream self-repair can make a genuine mediator dispensable under a single ablation. [McGrath et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15771) demonstrate compensatory computation after attention-layer ablations. A null single-head reversion therefore cannot establish absence of a route, while a successful sufficiency patch does not establish uniqueness.

First sweep **position families** rather than thousands of heads: first-table key sites, second-table key/value sites, question-name sites and final query site. The PATH-0004 suffix pooled several of these and cannot isolate them. Then use a discovery-only head/layer screen, preselect a small set, and evaluate frozen sites on new bundles. For an alleged edge, isolate the additive attention contribution from the relevant source positions to the recipient write, not just the entire recipient residual. Test QK-routing and OV-content modifications separately where the implementation permits exact recomputation. Replacing an additive source contribution while freezing the rest is a defined edge intervention; changing attention masks and renormalizing softmax is a **different** intervention. Distinguish direct edge effects from full downstream recomputation. [Anthropic's circuit-tracing methods](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/methods.html) explicitly treat attribution graphs as hypotheses requiring interventions in the original model; replacement models can miss nonlinear or indirect computation. Gradient attribution can prioritize candidates but should not substitute for exact patch runs, especially after [AtP*'s documented approximation failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00745).

### Controls that make a positive result interpretable

1. **Numerics and transport:** identity reproduces source logits; whole-prefix donor reproduces donor logits and full answer; exact replay of an unedited captured write is a no-op; position masks and tensor shapes are audited. Measure maximum full-logit error and save it even on early exits.
2. **Answer specificity:** cross \(F\) with independently varied \(G\), including an unseen output relabeling. A fixed donor-answer change across different \(G\) fails the intermediate-key hypothesis even if donor answer accuracy looks high on one table.
3. **Support and magnitude:** matched random vectors at the same sites and norms, unrelated/mismatched donor keys, patches from another lexical bundle, equal-size irrelevant positions or heads, and reverse-direction edits. Match per-position norms; random noise alone is a weak null for structured donor transplants.
4. **Route specificity:** patch first table, second table, question and final query separately; compare single mediator, selected mediators, all-head and all-position positive controls. Test both reversion and injection. Monitor whether ablated effects reappear in other downstream heads or MLPs before declaring necessity.
5. **Behavior preservation:** independent copy and noncomposition queries, clean accuracy, complete answer, first-token margin, and distributional divergence. A generic crash or changed output style is not a key transport mechanism.
6. **Statistical unit and selection:** register primary layer/site/metric after calibration, select heads only on discovery, estimate uncertainty by lexical bundle, test on disjoint confirmation bundles, and include failed/low-confidence clean records in a separate intention-to-test report. Multiple head/layer searches need an explicit selection ledger.

The original PATH-0001 single-block all-head positive control failed, and PATH-0003's all-block cut is an architectural/numerical control. Neither licenses a claim that a named head already mediates composed lookup. A selective path claim needs confirmation under the controls above and robustness to source/donor orientation and independent \(G\). Even then the explanation is of this model's known-answer computation, not of Voynich production.

## Relation to decipherment and prior methods

The Voynich corpus has already supported word co-occurrence and information-theoretic structure reports ([Montemurro and Zanette 2013](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0066344)), statistical/linguistic constraint analysis ([Reddy and Knight 2011](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511/)), and viable nonsemantic mimicry proposals ([Timm and Schinner 2020](https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2019.1596999); [Rugg and Taylor 2017](https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2016.1206753)). None of those observations gives oracle meanings to the manuscript's glyphs. Prior cipher work uses explicit channel and language assumptions: [Kambhatla et al. 2018](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1102/) compare neural-language-model search to n-gram methods on substitution ciphers, and [Knight et al. 2006](https://aclanthology.org/www.mt-archive.info/05/Coling-ACL-2006-Knight.pdf) study unsupervised decipherment under specified mapping models. Success under supplied English tables is therefore only **method qualification**. It must not be sold as manuscript semantics.

A valid bridge would train/evaluate candidate generators with known hidden states and with matched nonsemantic, cipher and linguistic controls; demonstrate that a recovered *causal intermediate or update rule* discriminates their held-out samples under family/key changes; then freeze that discriminator before testing held-out folios. The manuscript supplies neither oracle states nor interventions on its historical author. Model-internal paths can expose how a predictor processes Voynich text; they cannot by themselves prove how that text was produced. This is the project's [research protocol](../PROTOCOL.md) applied to mechanistic work.

## Decision rule for the next block

The most valuable bounded successor is **competence qualification plus factorial key-transfer**, then path localization only if it succeeds. Failure at clean competence means improve or replace the task/model, with a new held-out panel. Failure of the cross-\(G\) transfer despite high clean competence means the broad PATH-0004 crossover was compatible with answer injection or task-specific shortcut rather than a reusable key. Passing cross-\(G\) transfer but failing narrow mediation means we have functional composition without a localized path, and should search parallel recipient positions or distributed routes. Passing both directions of frozen mediator tests would justify a limited circuit claim for known-answer two-table lookup. None of these outcomes alone is a decipherment.
