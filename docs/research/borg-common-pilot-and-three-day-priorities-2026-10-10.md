# Fastest path to a constrained decipherment claim

This strategic audit follows the request to solve Voynich within three days. That date cannot be promised. We can use the deadline to end open-ended method qualification and obtain three decisive deliverables. No phrase of the Voynich manuscript has been deciphered by the current program.

## What the existing evidence actually says

The strongest existing global reader is not the latest exact sampler. `GLOBAL-KEY-READ-001` reports **625/7,168 letter edits (8.7193%)**; its interventions rescue one severe failure while worsening another. `BLIND-CHANNEL-CONFIRM-002` has fourteen near-oracle keys and two catastrophic fits; the source can prefer the correct dictionary while search misses it. Recent point-reading/shape campaigns recover no exact keys. Exact stationarity, cost arithmetic and source receipt correctness are useful engineering qualifications; they are not evidence of semantic recovery. The shape receipt's original reporting failure remains a failure even after its separate saved-receipt closure.

`NAIBBE-003` recovers136/138 keys with7/12,296 letter edits under supplied code tables, roles, lattices and source alphabet. This supports the source scorer in that assisted task, not unknown historical code-family discovery. `BORG-GLYPH-BRIDGE-005` preserves local ink ambiguities; atlas names are still not a globally verified historical cipher alphabet. None of those preparations has previously produced a sealed historical Borg key. These distinctions prevent a successful toy condition from quietly becoming a claimed manuscript result.

For Voynich itself, EXP-0039's previous-terminal→next-initial effect survives an independently sourced transcription on **exposed validation leaves**. EXP-0041's tested simple prose/homophony/transposition controls do not jointly reproduce the vocabulary/edge pattern; the richer EXP-0042 context result fails its cross-transcription gate. This is a promising channel constraint because a candidate explanation must reproduce a relationship visible in independent transcription, not because any letter value or meaning is known.

The old visual anchor route has not supplied a reliable semantic crib: ANCHOR-0001 fails its recurrence criterion; ANCHOR-0002 has nine potentially alignable groups and zero independently confirmed duplicates; HERBAL-CONTROL-003's three matching methods all miss the gate; fixed-position ZODIAC-0001 is at chance. Repeating arbitrary string metrics on those same labels would be another adaptive statistical report.

## Three finite deliverables

**Today: a sealed historical capability test.** [BORG-COMMON-001](../experiments/BORG-COMMON-001.md) uses conservative byte exclusions, one global injective key, frozen Latin counts, two new planted keys with previously exposed source authors, wrong-language/row-shuffle diagnostics and a physical-leaf transfer panel. The one producer fits at most15CPU minutes; the arithmetic audit at most100CPU seconds, $0. Only after the candidate seal should a separately registered phase acquire the published answer and reconcile the key with ink. If planted controls fail, the bottleneck is still search/model capability. If they pass but historical key validation fails, the ASCII/injectivity/source hypothesis is rejected or remains unresolved. If both pass, we acquire the first actual historical decoding validation—not a Voynich result—and can justify a bounded transfer attempt.

**Next24hours: use the strongest reader on a finite actual-Voynich channel challenge.** Keep whole EVA units and native word/line boundaries available; do not assume six glyphs are the manuscript or spaces are plaintext words. Register a small candidate family capable of carrying a terminal state into the next initial, a no-carry model and matched count/line-reset/null controls. Fit only train leaves; lock parameters before an already designated development transfer. Predict the independently observed edge constraint and compare full literal outputs and description length. The parallel paragraph-carry experiment can locate whether the dependency crosses physical lines; it cannot assign plaintext. A decisive negative rules out a specified carrier/reset mechanism; a positive narrows which channel transformations need a real reader. Do not resume infinite sampler admissions or enlarge a network just to improve loss.

**By72hours: either a fixed reading predicts an external constraint, or report an explicit obstruction.** A semantic proposal needs a single constrained key/grammar that predicts excluded material and external evidence. An image-based crib needs credible independent annotation first: prepare a small anonymous panel of the nine existing alignable groups for expert/user assessment without showing decoder guesses. The [manuscript's later annotations](https://voynich.nu/writing.html) are not automatically original Voynich plaintext or reliable month-name cribs. Without an independent anchor, symmetry and model flexibility may leave many equally good readings; quantify that ambiguity rather than naming a language from fluency. A clean report of which hypotheses were eliminated is useful progress, but it is not a solved manuscript.

## Why larger architectures do not cure the present obstruction

A latent label can be renamed without changing predictions; several different channels and source distributions can explain the same surface sequence. A sufficiently flexible language model can reward a plausible output invented by a flexible decoder. More parameters, cosine clusters and activation probes do not break those equivalences by themselves. Mechanistic interpretation becomes valuable **after** a model has a constrained, behaviorally verified reading task: intervene on an inferred carrier state, predict specific future characters, compare matched interventions and preserve one shared key on new text. Otherwise it explains the surrogate task we supplied.

This assessment is consistent with [Reddy & Knight2011](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511.pdf), whose distributional/boundary analyses are observations rather than a reading, and with the methodological language-prior approach in [Ravi & Knight2011](https://aclanthology.org/P11-1025.pdf). The [official Borg report](https://www.su.se/english/research/research-catalogue/research-projects/d/decipherment-of-historical-manuscripts/the-borg-cipher) supplies a real historical calibration opportunity. Recent [Rozanova & Temerev](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17096) and [Parisel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19762v2) abstract-level review reinforces careful unit/channel hypotheses; their claims are not our results, verified semantic anchors or a proof excluding every nonsemantic generator.

The urgent practical decision is to spend compute on a candidate that can be checked against a real answer, while the manuscript-facing work resolves a specified carrier boundary. Do not call source likelihood, key-space geometry, positive probes or engineered mathematical correctness decipherment.
