## What the evidence says to change

The recent complete-reading and local-repair campaigns recovered **zero exact
positive records**. Their source, probability, and implementation audits passed,
but that does not make them our strongest decoder. The latest shape-only
qualification completed sixteen panels and then failed while combining receipt
counters. Its failure is preserved; a separately registered receipt closure
will account for the saved panels without rerunning them.

The older [unknown-key confirmation](../experiments/BLIND-CHANNEL-CONFIRM-002-results.md)
has stronger recovery evidence: fourteen of sixteen keys were near the
supplied-key reader, while two catastrophic fits defeated the full gate.
[Global dictionary search](../experiments/GLOBAL-KEY-READ-001-results.md) rescued
one catastrophic case but worsened the other; overall error remained 8.72% and
exact records fell from ten to nine. This is a useful comparator and an
unreliable solver. A new architecture must beat it on fresh, matched controls
before it earns a long training run.

The strongest manuscript constraint is the previous-terminal/next-initial
association replicated in [two transcriptions](../experiments/EXP-0039-results.md).
Those transcriptions describe the same manuscript and the validation leaves
were repeatedly exposed. A [richer context model](../experiments/EXP-0042-results.md)
failed its cross-transcription gate. Existing plant and zodiac anchor tests
also failed. There is currently no verified semantic crib.

## Primary-method check

[Reddy and Knight, 2011, §§5.2 and 6.2](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511.pdf)
already discuss end-to-start dependence and unusual line-edge distributions.
That is why the new test predicts a distinction between line continuation and
paragraph reset instead of presenting the boundary association as a discovery.
Their interpretations do not establish a particular coding mechanism.

[Kambhatla and colleagues, 2018, §§2–4](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1102.pdf)
use whole-message neural scores to rank shared substitution hypotheses. Their
character model is trained on a large known-English corpus, with additional
Zodiac writing, and their channel family is supplied. This supports whole-key
search as an architectural direction; it does not establish language discovery
or transfer to Voynich. Our initial Borg pilot uses an explicit, finite-order
Latin source and a narrower injective mapping. Its outcomes determine whether
that instrument warrants a more expensive neural or homophonic extension.

## Where larger models and mechanistic interpretation belong

World/action models, discrete denoising and recurrent global inference remain
possible proposal mechanisms. The failed append model often relied on supplied
bindings and external legality masks; scaling that interface does not add
global key revision. The new symbolic marginalization removes sampled-key
noise but does not guarantee useful transport or recovery.

Choose an architecture around a demonstrated missing operation: propose an
entire dictionary, revise a distant shared binding, or infer a bounded coding
state. First measure known-answer recovery and source-family transfer. Then
causal interventions can test whether a learned state is necessary for the
recovered behavior, using held-out keys, matched patches and rescue controls.
Latent cosine structure or activation changes alone do not establish this.
The existing [repair proposal](conditional-world-action-program-2026-10-10.md)
is a hypothesis, not evidence that a larger trained model will solve Voynich.

The operational change is to spend compute on concrete decoding predictions
and historical checks. Correct arithmetic, plausible Latin, and interpretable
features remain intermediate evidence; none is called decipherment.
