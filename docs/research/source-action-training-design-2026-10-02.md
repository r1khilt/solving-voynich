# Reading-action training: explicit dictionary memory versus a matched control

This is an exploratory inverse-competence comparison, not a Voynich reading or a mechanistic circuit result. The earlier whole-key and search campaigns did not recover their known answers. Their completed audits and known-key diagnostics motivate teaching the inverse to read an observation while building its dictionary, instead of predicting 23 dictionary entries in isolation.

## Method review and architectural rationale

The primary decipherment review, assumptions, prior Voynich work and links are preserved in [source-action inverse](source-action-inverse-2026-10-02.md). Corlett and Penn (2010), Hauer, Hayward and Kondrak (2014), and Kambhatla, Born and Sarkar (2023) motivate combining source constraints and decipherment search. Their spacing, cipher-family and supervision assumptions do not establish this variable-unit inverse. Our earlier whole-key, fragment, source-state and particle methods already exist; this is not a claim to have invented those methods. The [cache review](source-action-cached-inference-2026-10-02.md) explains the unchanged causal attention calculation and its qualification.

The model encodes exactly two observed records. At each scheduled reading position it chooses a source row and whether to consume one or two observed glyphs. An unbound row receives that observed unit; future uses must reproduce it literally. Explicit pair embeddings preserve which row was bound to which unit. Past actions, offsets, record identity and previously visited dictionary entries are available; the current target, future source letters, true source length, unvisited dictionary rows and hidden unit boundaries are not features. Record identity is intentional and breaks record-order invariance. Repeated dictionary units are allowed. Unused rows stay unbound.

The matched control has the same 96,039,982 parameters, initialization, source stream, supervision, optimizer and legal-action grammar, but its explicit dictionary-memory query input is zero. It can still infer bindings from previous actions and the common legal mask. Therefore a benefit measures the usefulness of *explicit neural access* to past dictionary memory, not the absence of all binding information. Neither a benefit nor an attention pattern by itself identifies a circuit.

## Data and identifiability

Reuse only the audited twelve-author Latin training pool and filtered Pliny development pool from JOINT-KEY-TRAIN-001. Retain the original source versions, body/edition checks, author roles, segment boundaries and 64-letter overlap checks; reopen their approved inputs to reproduce the prepared text hashes. Pliny is already exposed development data, not a fresh author or historical holdout. No manuscript panel or reserved author is read.

Allocate 64 development episodes with seed 92221, each two distinct sampled windows (overlap remains possible within the development pool), source lengths independently uniform 64–224 and a uniform 23-row key over the 42 non-erasing one/two-glyph units. Exclude both raw and canonical keys from the old 64-episode pool. Require 64 distinct new raw and canonical keys; fail rather than reseed on collision. Training excludes the union of both old and new development raw/canonical keys, using the existing finite 16-draw rejection bound. Fresh keys do not make exposed source passages into independent test text. There is no source-language or mechanism-family transfer claim.

For fixed unknown keys this channel can be nonidentifiable: duplicate units and alternative segmentations can encode different source strings as the same cipher. Two length-64 all-zero records admit both all-row-0 and all-row-1 source strings under the all-single-zero dictionary. Preserve this explicit unrecoverable witness. Add the first 16 development ciphers shuffled separately within each record after its last first-occurrence position, preserving exact counts, lengths and canonical symbol names, plus 16 IID six-glyph controls of the same lengths with first-occurrence canonicalization, seed 92251, and the ambiguity witness. Their 33 greedy paths are diagnostic null outputs at every checkpoint. A completed legal path on such a control is not successful decipherment or proof of language. Conditional q(reading | cipher) is not a marginal probability of the cipher or a language-family classifier. No null threshold is selected from their outputs.

## Objective, controls and predeclared decision

Fit seeds 92203 and 92209, each explicit memory on/off, in fixed order: binding-92203, no-binding-92203, binding-92209, no-binding-92209. Each receives 20,000 updates, batch four, 80,000 independently drawn training episodes, no early stopping or extension. CPU random source stream is seeded seed+100003 independently of Torch and neural decisions. The paired source stream and initial full weights must match exactly after fitting.

Use mean *whole reading-path* negative log probability, with no target-length normalization, AdamW (weight decay .01, foreach disabled), gradient clipping 1, learning rate linear 200-step warmup to .0003 then cosine decay to .00003. Encoder width 768, 12 heads, eight encoder layers, four decoder layers, feedforward factor four, maximum 448 observed glyphs per record, two records, no dropout. Refer to the frozen actual configuration for every field. Five checkpoints at 0, 1000, 4000, 10000 and 20000; select smallest mean development whole-path NLL, tie to earlier step. Include step zero to preserve a non-learning baseline. This score differs in units from the earlier whole-key proposal loss and cannot be compared directly.

At every checkpoint separately generate a deterministic free greedy reading. The finite non-erasing path has at most the total observed glyph count (up to 896 actions). No restart, beam repair, target-length stopping or dictionary completion. Report source-letter edit errors, exact records, matches on actually used generating rows, complete used mappings, completion and failure counts. Every dead end receives the full true-letter edit penalty, zero used-row matches and zero exact readings. Ignore unused generating key rows. Gold source/key enter scoring only. The stored greedy-path log probability is its probability under the stochastic base model; it is not the output law of deterministic greedy decoding or a whole-key marginal.

For each seed the explicit-memory benefit gate requires all of: at least one nat lower selected whole-path NLL per episode, at least five percentage points higher used-row match fraction, at least 10% fewer penalized edits, at least as many complete paths, at least one exact source record, and at least one complete used mapping. Require both seeds for replicated *exploratory* benefit. Retain all checkpoints and both seeds, including failures. These effect thresholds are prospective engineering gates, not confidence intervals or a formal population significance claim. A single fit, loss gain without recovery, or good null output does not pass them. General inverse qualification, causal circuit identification, historical channel/language selection and decipherment remain false regardless of the result.

## Actual audit and mechanistic follow-up

Before fitting, one preparation and one input audit reproduce source identities, allocated keys, exact windows and target compilation. All new code, design and admission evidence must be committed, pushed and verified at the exact remote revision before preparation; then prepared compact inputs/audits must be frozen and pushed before fitting.

One completion audit checks every checkpoint hash/config/finite float32 tensor/digest, every episode ledger and sampling seed, all 80,000 updates in total, source exposures, checkpoint selection, failed terminal processes, resources and paired initial/data digests. It independently recomputes literal readings and a separate dynamic-programming edit distance for all checkpoint outputs and null controls. Initial and selected checkpoints additionally receive full CPU-double replay of every allocated true path and saved greedy prefix, including control prefixes. Maximum whole-path probability difference .01 nat; selected greedy action deficit against CPU argmax at most .0002; every checkpoint's short updated GPU/CPU-double legal-logit comparison at most .002. These are numeric tolerances, not certificate intervals. The auditor shares PyTorch and the same author; it does not replay all optimizer updates or claim an independent expert review.

Only after adequate inverse competence would fresh, prospectively allocated causal interventions become useful: remove or swap row-unit bindings with matched grammar-preserving controls, compare behavior with both seeds, and test specific decoder components. Large training is a means to make those experiments interpretable. A memory-on benefit alone is an architectural ablation result, not neuron-level causality. If competence fails, inspect the retained error inventory and hypothesis before another registered objective; never silently extend these fits.
