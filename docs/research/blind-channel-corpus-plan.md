# Blind-channel Latin corpus pilot: metadata and partition plan

Reviewed 2026-09-27. **Metadata-only planning, not a corpus freeze or experiment registration.** No new full corpus was downloaded, no final-fit/transfer text was opened, and no synthetic task, fit, or score was produced. This narrows the first engineering pilot in [the blind-channel design](blind-channel-recovery-design.md); it does not qualify that design's proposed three-language, 37-task benchmark. ## Extraction, orthography, and isolation rules to freeze

Use one author in each role even when a volume contains multiple works. Keep all editions, alternate names and translations of that author in the same role or exclude them. English/Italian arms must not use translations of these Latin works. Exclude multi-author anthologies and cross-role quotations, including quotations hidden in editorial notes. Catalogue-level author separation is necessary but cannot certify passage-level independence.

Before acquiring F/T, finish and freeze extraction policy on P1/P2/D and appoint an evaluator path that does not expose final plaintext to the solver. The evaluator may then perform mechanical body/rights/duplicate checks, retain private exact extraction spans and hashes, and return only eligible counts, contamination flags and pass/fail decisions. If Latin bodies cannot be separated unambiguously, stop and revise the source plan before generation; never choose a replacement because a decoder struggled.

Proposed orthographic policy: preserve original raw bytes; use Unicode normalization, lowercase, explicitly expand Latin ligatures, remove length marks, and map `j→i` and `v→u` for one declared classical alphabet. Treat punctuation, digits, page/section numbers, editorial brackets, Greek quotations and apparatus explicitly before alphabet filtering; silently deleting non-ASCII characters is unacceptable. Keep a token-boundary map in evaluator data even if the pilot plaintext alphabet omits spaces. This is a lossy engineering convention, not a claim about ancient writing or Voynich. Exact codepoints, expansion tables, inclusion rules and hashes still need development-only verification and registration.

Edition spelling, `u/v`, `i/j`, accents/macrons, ligatures, Greek insertions, elision, abbreviations and modern punctuation can make an edition identifiable without revealing an underlying author or cipher. Standardizing a subset does not remove all edition or genre effects. P2 is verse while P1/D/F/T are prose works; report source-only selection scores separately by author and do not label this a genre-controlled sample.

An evaluator-only duplicate audit must compare normalized passages across all five bodies and any later English/Italian candidates, as well as detect exact duplicates within roles. Freeze window-overlap length and exclusion thresholds using P1/P2/D; do not invent thresholds after reading F/T. Long quotations shared by ancient authors can survive removal of modern notes. Record excluded intervals and reasons, not merely a final assertion of no leakage. Do not derive source priors, normalization choices, stopping probabilities or search proposals from F/T character statistics.

## Concrete next pilot, still unrun

1. Acquire and audit **P1/P2/D only**, each with a 2 MiB raw-file cap; stop rather than silently truncate an oversized file. Pin URL, retrieval time, raw and derived hashes, rights notice, body spans, code version and exact counts. Estimate an order-0/1 source model on P1, select its bounded smoothing/stopping configuration on P2, and freeze a final prior from P1/P2 only. Cap each prior author's contribution at 50,000 eligible letters by a predeclared rule; measure sufficiency rather than assuming it from file size.
2. Use disjoint Cicero development blocks for **four exposed positive tasks: A/B × two independent keys**, with four fitting and two transfer records per task, each underlying record 192–256 letters. Add four paired nonlanguage tasks and one explicit ambiguity fixture. These nine development tasks are a proposed throughput/correctness exercise, not the sealed benchmark. No family-C examples are generated or consulted. Exact lengths and emission boundaries remain hidden from the blind arm even though the developmental answers are available to the evaluator.
3. Compare blind scoring with channel/structure/unit oracles and the fixed degenerate/nonlanguage baselines. First measure at most one CPU-hour, two concurrent workers and 8 GiB aggregate memory, with no paid compute or API use. Failure to fit the budget is a development result. Stop on inference disagreement, accidental gold access, or insufficient disjoint body text; do not compensate by taking F/T excerpts.
4. Freeze architecture, extraction rules, seeds/streams, budgets and numerical recovery/false-declaration gates from development evidence before any final generation. Only then permit evaluator-only F/T acquisition and complete a separate final registration. Keep all inferred final channels frozen before transfer answers are opened. Do not call this Latin-only pilot a source-language-identification test: Latin is its sole candidate.

## Remaining blocks

The metadata supports this five-author allocation, but acquisition/extraction/hash manifests are incomplete for four authors; final editions contain English material; cross-author quotation/duplicate checks are unrun; orthographic maps and exact body spans are not frozen; and no runtime or recovery threshold has been calibrated. The original Italian/English allocations and full 37-task matrix remain **unverified and unregistered**. This plan supplies a bounded next acquisition/development step, not evidence for unknown-channel recovery or decipherment.
