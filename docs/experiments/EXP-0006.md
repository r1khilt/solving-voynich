# EXP-0006 — Learn a causal state intervention, with controls for misleading steering

Registered 2026-09-20 PDT / 2026-09-21 UTC before new synthetic contexts, alignment fitting or outcomes. Follow-up to the failed EXP-0004 probe-direction intervention. Supervised methodological calibration (M-001/M-003), not a Voynich decoding attempt. All existing language-model weights remain frozen; manuscript test remains unscored.

## Background reviewed before implementation

Existing Voynich, cipher and synthetic-control precedents are in [PRIOR_WORK](../research/PRIOR_WORK.md). [Geiger et al., Finding Alignments Between Interpretable Causal Variables and Distributed Neural Representations, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.02536v3), sections3–4 and appendixD, optimize distributed interchange alignments with frozen models and orthogonal transformations, including random-network checks. Our low-rank projected optimization is DAS-inspired, not a replication of their tasks or parametrization.

[Makelov, Lange, Geiger and Nanda, ICLR2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/70b8505ac79e3e131756f793cd80eb8d-Abstract-Conference.html) warn that effective subspace steering can misidentify the natural computation. [Wu et al.'s reply](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12631) disputes parts of the characterization/evaluation. We preserve that disagreement and test controls rather than treating steering as proof. Primary method text and critique/reply abstracts accessed 2026-09-20 PDT.

## Data and fixed language models

- Same known cycle/null generator as EXP-0004: four states, two symbols per state, probability0.65 state advance/signal; otherwise state-preserving uniform-symbol null. Bayesian state posterior is a three-degree-of-freedom predictive belief, not necessarily a single true-state label recoverable without uncertainty.
- Freeze EXP-0004 cycle/null best models for seeds42/43/44. Add the seed42 initial/untrained checkpoint as a negative control. No language-model retraining and no Voynich weights/text.
- Fresh synthetic prefixes with master seed6201, separate child RNG streams: 4,096 alignment-fit and1,024 validation prefixes of128 symbols;1,024 test128 prefixes;512 test96 and512 test192 prefixes. Assert no duplicate full contexts across these splits and no fresh context appears as an exact substring of old EXP-0004 structured corpus pages. Track exact generated bytes/digests. These are new contexts within the same generator/alphabet, not new mechanism families.
- Fit/validation/test interchange pairs come only from their respective pools: same final observed symbol, different posterior-MAP states, both confidence>=0.6; recipient page order, seeded random qualifying donor, at most2,048/512/512 pairs at128 and256 at each other length. Report counts/donor reuse. Preserve the no-pair outcome instead of adaptive substitutions.
- Separate test128 preservation pairs: same final symbol and MAP state, both confidence>=0.6, closest other posterior with L1 distance<=0.05; at most256 recipients. Measure changes to their original model distribution. Pairs are selected by generator beliefs, never by model behavior.

## Learned interventions and controls

- Sites: final query position after block0 (early) and block1 (late), both residual width128. Learn an orthonormal128×3 basis U; replace `r` with `r + ((donor-r) U) Uᵀ` at that position. Backpropagate through the frozen remaining model.
- Adam lr0.01, batch64, exactly400 alignment updates, QR retraction after each update to keep columns orthonormal. Seed fixed from model seed/site; true and shuffled conditions share initialization and sampled pairs. Validate every50, retain lowest validation cross-entropy basis. Fixed local budget:14 fits /5,600 alignment updates (three trained models × two sites × true/shuffled objectives, plus untrained seed42 × two sites × true objective). Estimated under20 minutes, at most a few GB RAM through activation caching, no paid API.
- Objective: match the donor's Bayesian next-letter distribution (condition on emitting one of the eight ordinary letters). This explicitly uses generator supervision; it does not infer an unknown algorithm. Shuffled-supervision controls randomly permute desired distributions across fit pairs and separately validation pairs, with fixed seeds; select on their assigned shuffled validation target, then evaluate actual counterfactual fidelity on fresh test.
- Test each basis on all three lengths, plus preservation pairs. Controls: unchanged recipient; whole residual at that position; eight seeded random rank-three subspaces with the learned intervention's per-example displacement norm; shuffled-objective learned basis; centered four-category readout-weight span (top3 SVD directions of pair-averaged effective unembedding weights). The readout span helps detect trivial output manipulation, particularly at the late site.
- Record full-donor model predictions as a reference distinct from the oracle. Early whole-residual patch need not reproduce donor predictions because other prefix activations remain from the recipient. Late whole-residual patch must reproduce donor logits. Do not confuse these controls.

## Measurements, decision rule and limits

Primary: mean KL bits from donor Bayesian next-letter distribution to intervened model distribution, with per-pair values; lower is better. Also report distance to donor-model distribution, argmax next-state-category agreement, displacement norms, preserved-posterior KL changes, selected fit steps, and all seeds/lengths/controls. Donor reuse and shared pools make simple independent-pair significance claims inappropriate; use descriptive statistics.

Practical success threshold, not a significance test: at a given site, true alignment reduces test128 oracle KL by>=0.10 bits from unchanged and beats both mean norm-matched random and shuffled-supervision alignment by>=0.05 bits in all three trained seeds. Check whether the untrained model or trivial readout span achieves comparable steering before interpreting success. Report failures and contrary lengths. Even success establishes a useful supervised intervention under these tests, not a unique minimal circuit, faithful full algorithm or historical process.

Cache clean activations to avoid recomputing frozen early layers. Validate resumed-forward parity and intervention gradient equivalence against the complete hook-based model; identity, orthogonality, frozen weights, split/pair constraints, and late full-donor reproduction must pass (logit tolerance1e-5). If compute/memory or numerical controls fail, stop and document any necessary implementation correction before rerunning; do not retune ranks, sites, thresholds or budgets on test results. New synthetic test is scored only after each fixed fit and is not used to revise that fit.
