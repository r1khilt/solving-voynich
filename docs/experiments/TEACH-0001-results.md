# TEACH-0001 result: randomized teacher did not qualify

**Registered verdict: NOT QUALIFIED.** The primary teacher learned literal copy (256/256 on the held-out control) but achieved only 127/256 = 49.61% on jointly held-out two-table composition, 134/256 = 52.34% on held-out direct lookup, and **0/128 exact** four-way factorial quartets. All six predeclared qualification clauses failed. No mechanistic intervention on this teacher is justified as a test of a *successful* composed-lookup circuit. This result does not change PATH-0004, PATH-0006 or PATH-0007 and is not evidence about a Voynich reading.

## Frozen run and controls

The run used source revision `1d66cffad9827200ccaf6fcd5d17667f0d6b9f1b`, the registered `TeacherConfig`, PyTorch 2.14.0 on local MPS, and the command `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m voynich.workspace.teacher1_train --device mps`. Each 664,704-parameter arm completed 2,500 steps × batch 128. The primary and independent-composed-label null used the same input stream and initialization seed. Total measured time was **109.21 seconds** under the 3,600-second ceiling. Peak **sampled PyTorch MPS allocation** was 10,880,512 bytes under the 8 GiB bound; this is not total process or system memory. The native-versus-hooked numerical gate's maximum logit error was `1.7881393432617188e-07`; finite loss and gradients passed. No paid API, downloaded model, manuscript training or final-test score was involved.

Episode intervals are descriptive: the frozen suite includes two duplicate full episodes in the jointly held-out composed cell and two in the factorial cell, and multiple episodes share table families. The four members of a quartet are explicitly scored together. The primary-minus-null jointly held-out accuracy is **16.41 percentage points**, far below the required 60 points. The null's 33.20% exceeds its ≤25% gate. The positive symbolic oracle independently parsed every evaluation label correctly; unlike the neural teacher, its answer follows both tables by construction.

## What the near-half scores suggest—and do not prove

The primary's last-100-step training loss was **0.6387 nats**, effectively flat from step 1,500 (0.6395). A hypothetical strategy that perfectly copies on 10% of episodes and makes a 50/50 guess between the two visible second-table objects on the remaining 90% has expected mixture loss \(0.9\ln2=0.6238\) nats. The measured curve is close to that simple strategy. The null's last loss **1.6033** is likewise close to \(0.7\ln8+0.2\ln2=1.5942\): uniform random eight-way labels for composed episodes, two-way guesses for direct queries, and perfect copying. These are **diagnostic reference calculations**, not a fit of the model's per-task probabilities or a proof of its algorithm. The 49–54% primary composition/direct accuracies, lack of an F/G-train advantage, perfect copy, and 0 exact quartets are consistent with learning to find or copy visible candidate objects while failing the key-conditioned choice. Without saved predictions or an independent forward pass, we cannot establish that exact shortcut, distinguish confidence from accuracy, or locate the failure in attention versus optimization.

The null's 33.20% on the jointly held-out composed cell exceeds the uniform-eight chance level of 12.5%. That does **not** by itself show data leakage: the null still received true direct and copy labels on structurally similar prompts, and its predictions may favor visible table objects even while composed labels are random. The null's aggregate training loss is near the reference for random composed labels, and the audit found no held-out-family leakage or label error. Its behavior remains unexplained at the prediction level. The direct-lookup test uses G-holdout families, so its near-half score does not alone tell whether direct lookup worked on G-train families; the report lacks that separate direct evaluation. More identical training steps are a weak first response given the flat loss, but the run does not prove that longer training could never help.

## Bounded successor hypothesis, not a new result

A sharper next qualification would explicitly teach the **first hop** before expecting composition: introduce a first-table-only name→key query, then direct key→object queries, then mixed two-table queries, optionally with an auxiliary key prediction at a declared intermediate layer. Compare that curriculum against a same-compute version of the original mixture and keep an independent-label null. Freeze fresh F/G family partitions and episode seeds, report both in-family and held-out direct/first-hop competence, and retain the four-way factorial gate before any mechanistic interpretation. A short throughput pilot on the measured 109-second run could bound a two- or three-seed comparison within a predeclared local time and MPS ceiling. This is a **hypothesis and proposed design**, not an executed improvement; any threshold or architecture change requires a new registration. The purpose remains qualification of a synthetic method, not a historical claim.
