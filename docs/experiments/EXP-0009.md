# EXP-0009 — Does a causal intervention change several future predictions?

Registered before new data, discovery scores or confirmation scores. Frozen-model methodological calibration plus exploratory manuscript localization. This is one independent track of CAMPAIGN-0001. No language-model training, paid APIs or manuscript final-test scoring.

## Question and motivation

EXP-0006 could steer the toy model's immediate answer, but a direct output-weight projection worked equally well. A stronger requirement is to intervene **only in an earlier prefix** and correctly change predictions after the model subsequently reads the same new observations. Does any individual early head or MLP component mediate those later consequences, reliably across independently trained models and fresh examples?

This tests the causal-method hypotheses M-001/M-003. It does not infer that the manuscript uses our toy state machine, identify a historical cipher, or establish a minimal complete circuit.

## Data and separation

### Synthetic calibration

Use the existing cycle/null generator unchanged: four states, two symbols per state, 0.65 signal probability and 0.35 ambiguous uniform filler probability. Freeze the EXP-0004 trained best checkpoints for seeds 42/43/44; add seed-42 initial weights as an untrained negative control. No diagnostic fitting or label-trained probe is used in this experiment.

Generate independent 128-character prefix pools: discovery 4,096 (seed 9201), confirmation 8,192 (9202). Require every prefix to be unique across these pools and absent from every same-length substring in the old structured EXP-0004 corpus and EXP-0006 pools. This is new text from the **same generator/alphabet**, not a family/key holdout; broader recovery belongs to the parallel EXP-0008 track.

Select 512 discovery and 1,024 confirmation pairs in recipient order. Recipient and correct donor have the same last symbol, posterior confidence at least 0.6, and different Bayesian MAP states; choose a seeded random eligible donor. The mismatched donor has the recipient's MAP state and last symbol and minimizes posterior L1 distance among eligible other prefixes. No neural score is involved. Require full counts or stop rather than adapt selection.

Sample seven continuation observations from the correct donor's Bayesian posterior and known generator, using a separate continuation RNG (pair seed plus 1,000,000); append **exactly the same observed continuation** to recipient, correct donor and wrong donor. Pair identities are invariant to changes in suffix generation. At horizons 1, 2, 4 and 8, score each next-symbol prediction against the donor's exact Bayesian distribution conditioned on the observations revealed so far. The model never sees generator labels/oracle probabilities as input. Subsequent observations can erase the initial difference; report all horizons and the clean-donor floor rather than silently selecting persistent cases.

### Manuscript development analysis

Freeze EXP-0002 compact best checkpoints for seeds 42/43/44, with exact tokenizer/corpus hash checks. Use only the existing 24 validation pages. Randomly partition their ten physical leaf groups, five discovery/five confirmation (seed 9220); no physical leaf crosses these partitions. This prevents within-experiment site selection on confirmation leaves, but does **not** create a historically untouched holdout: earlier experiments already examined this validation corpus.

Choose up to 64 eligible positions per page with a full 128-unit prefix and eight subsequent units. All four horizon targets must be eligible and within the same page. Correct donor is the original prefix; recipient shuffles its remote 112 units while preserving local 16. Wrong donor is a training-page prefix sampled at stride 16, matched only on its final token. The original seven subsequent units are shared by all three conditions. Preserve uncertainty as visible context but exclude unknown, uncertainty, BOS and EOS prediction targets. Retain exact page/leaf/position coordinates and raw input arrays in ignored outputs.

## Interventions and discovery rule

For each two-layer/four-head model, map all 48 combinations:

- Eight projected head contributions, two MLP output writes and two post-block residual streams.
- Four prefix regions: final position; recent eight; remote 120; all 128. Overlapping regions are intentional and results are not additive.

Only coordinates within the original prefix may be patched. No future query activation is replaced. Standard donor replacement uses frozen clean donor activations. The same complete 48-site map is evaluated on discovery and confirmation for all three trained seeds, plus the untrained synthetic model. Head indices are not assumed to mean the same thing across seeds.

**Before scoring confirmation**, select exactly one site per model: the first-layer head/MLP site with lowest discovery primary loss averaged over horizons **2 and 4**, weighted equally across groups (synthetic recipient IDs or manuscript physical leaves). Ties use the lexical site name. Residual streams, second-layer sites and immediate-answer horizon 1 cannot be selected. Persist the choice before confirmation. All non-selected confirmation maps are exploratory.

On the chosen site, confirmation includes correct donor, wrong donor, four seeded norm-matched random directions, and zeroing. Random changes match correct donor displacement norm separately at every intervened position. For head outputs, random directions are projected into that head's actual output-weight column space before norm matching. This avoids comparing a legal head contribution with an arbitrary residual-space direction. Random perturbations remain distribution-shifting controls, not guaranteed natural activations.

Zeroing is performed on **clean donor inputs**; damage relative to clean donor describes necessity under this ablation, not corruption restoration. Whole-prefix embedding replacement is the full positive restoration control. Final-prefix-position rank-three readout-weight projection is the output-only control: four category-averaged effective output rows for synthetic, centered vocabulary output rows for manuscript. No alignment basis is learned here.

## Primary measure and falsifiable threshold

- Synthetic primary: KL bits from desired Bayesian next-symbol probabilities to the intervened model's distribution, conditioning on the eight ordinary symbols. Lower is better.
- Manuscript primary: true-target negative log probability in bits. No ground-truth causal state exists.
- Secondary for both: KL bits from clean donor-model predictions to intervened predictions, reported at every horizon. Absolute effects avoid unstable normalized recovery denominators.

For the **one discovery-selected site per seed**, require confirmation gain at **both horizons 2 and 4** to be at least 0.02 bits relative to unchanged recipient, and at least 0.01 bits better than both mean random and wrong-donor controls, in **all three trained seeds**. These are registered practical thresholds, not significance tests. Apply the same thresholds to the untrained synthetic model and report that negative control explicitly. Even passing supports future causal mediation under this setup, not full latent-state recovery. If selected sites differ across seeds, report that variation rather than relabeling heads to imply identity.

Report sample and equal-group mean scores; manuscript group means retained in compact reports, all per-example results stored in compressed ignored arrays. Five confirmation leaf groups are too few to justify strong population or token-independence claims. Donor reuse likewise limits independence in synthetic pairs. No new architecture/model selection follows from confirmation outcomes within this experiment.

## Integrity, budget and execution

Unit tests verify prefix-coordinate isolation, suffix-independent donor selection and prefix caches, individual-position norm matching and head-subspace constraints, Bayesian continuation indexing, selection isolation, threshold conjunction, whole-prefix restoration and output-only inability to influence future positions. Every evaluated batch checks the latter architectural invariant; first batches verify identity, whole-prefix embedding restoration, and full final-prefix residual restoration of the immediate answer to maximum absolute logit error below 1e-5. All models remain evaluation-mode, gradients disabled, state dictionaries verified unchanged.

Seven model jobs total: three trained synthetic, one untrained synthetic, three manuscript; each maps discovery and confirmation plus selected controls. Batch 32, two CPU threads, MPS per-process memory fraction 0.23 (about 11.9 GiB of this machine's reported recommended working set), maximum two hours wall time, no paid API.
