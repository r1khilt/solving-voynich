# HERBAL-CONTROL-0003: the disjoint-class open-set gate fails

The fixed 72-class historical-herbal panel does **not** qualify any of the three registered methods for Voynich-facing use. The primary Apple Vision method selected on development scores **0.4514** held-out macro balanced accuracy; frozen DINOv2 scores **0.5278**, and the folio-order-only challenger **0.4792**. An all-reject rule scores **0.5** on this balanced metric. All three methods fail the preregistered `≥0.80` feasibility gate and the separate two-source and one-to-one benefit gates. Independent scoring audits reproduce every decision. This is a negative historical chapter-matching result, not a decipherment result.

## Failure pattern and interpretation

The primary Vision rule rejects **63/72** known queries, assigns eight to the wrong known class, and correctly names one. The selected folio rule rejects **68/72** known queries, assigns three wrongly, and names one correctly. DINO is less collapsed: it names 24, rejects 32 and assigns 16 to the wrong class; it falsely accepts 10 of 36 unknown queries. Its known performance drops from 40/72 on development to 24/72 on evaluation. That explains most of the development-to-evaluation balanced-accuracy drop. DINO's higher known count than the metadata control is a reason to investigate its cues, not evidence that it recognizes botanical species: text, layout, color and manuscript conventions remain available in many crops.

For completeness, the **unselected** DINO `single_first` rule would have scored 0.5903 on evaluation. Selecting it now would use the held-out data to change the method; it cannot rescue the prospective `two_min` result or its failed gate. Similarly, the old 18-crop DINO 36/36 remains post-result exploratory and was matched by a page-order-only shortcut. The new panel's folio challenger fails too, but that does not turn the DINO failure into a pass.

Manual crop review exposed a category problem that a scalar score hides: Casanatense's *Canna* entry is a framed reed-harvest scene; the adjacent standalone reed is under a different *Canna mellis* entry. The frozen crop uses the scene, rather than the visually convenient neighbor. Egerton *ameos* has uncertain positional attribution, some chapters have multiple variants, and many boxes overlap text or other plants. These are genuine properties of this source-derived task and limit any morphology-specific claim. A future cue-isolation or improved model study needs a new prospectively registered holdout, independent chapter-image review and explicit handling of scenes/variants; retuning on this exposed evaluation split would not be a fresh test.

All three independent evaluation score audits return `pass` and reproduce the failed gates. The compact results and hashes are under `results/HERBAL-CONTROL-0003/`; raw JPEGs and crop PNGs remain Git-ignored but are linked by the committed input manifests. No Voynich image was assigned a chapter, no reserved Voynich text was opened or scored, and no word-level decipherment follows from this control.
