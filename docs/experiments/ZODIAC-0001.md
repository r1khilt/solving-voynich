# ZODIAC-0001: does zodiac-label spelling follow position across signs?

**Status:** exploratory registration, before any label-form/ordinal score. **Question:** If the approximately 30 labeled figures around each zodiac sign are numbered degrees, days, or a recurring positional catalogue, does a label's form contain a component that transfers to the same position on another sign? A positive result would identify only a recurrent positional code. It would not identify a number word, a star name, plaintext, or a historical source.

## Prior work and operational hypothesis

[Zandbergen's label survey](https://www.voynich.nu/extra/labels.html) documents 299 surviving zodiac labels and warns that labels could be names, numbers, coordinates, or something else; most exact forms are singletons. [Averyanov's independent zodiac study](https://voynich.site/en/hypotheses/zodiac-degrees/) proposes degree-list structure but finds an image-attribute signal local to Pisces, Aries, and Taurus and reports no reading of the labels. Its broader sigla/anti-numeral interpretation is a hypothesis, not adopted here. [Rozanova and Temerev (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17096) argue from ZL3b and matched controls that an EVA glyph need not be a plaintext letter or a blank-delimited form a lexical word; their multi-symbol-unit finding motivates 2- and 3-character patterns rather than exact-word matching. [Knight et al. (2006)](https://aclanthology.org/P06-2065/) illustrate that decipherment requires an explicit channel and external evidence, neither of which this position assay supplies.

The **restrictive prediction** is that IVTFF `Lz` transcription order within a complete 30-label diagram is a comparable ordinal index across signs. This is only a proxy for physical degree order: rings may start at different angles, traverse different directions, or encode something other than degree number. Testing a fixed proxy rather than searching rotations avoids fitting a favorable alignment after seeing labels. A failure therefore constrains the proxy and the chosen spelling representation, not all possible numeric schemes.

## Null, gate, and limits

Enumerate all 30×30 independent cyclic rotations of the **observed label-to-position assignment** on the two validation diagrams. Keep each diagram's label sequence, missing labels, and all 30 score vectors fixed; only its assigned ordinal moves by the chosen rotation. This preserves within-diagram adjacency and formulaic drift. The exact conditional one-sided probability is the proportion of 900 assignments with mean rank fraction at least the unrotated observation. The null is conditional on these two adjacent diagrams and fixed train prototypes, not a population-level claim about zodiac signs.

Call `positional_form_candidate` only if each validation diagram has at least 25 eligible labels, the primary mean fraction is at least 0.60, the exact probability is at most 0.05, and each diagram's mean is at least 0.55. Otherwise record `no_support_for_fixed_ordinal_proxy`. Do not tune n-gram range, IDF, cyclic offsets, inclusion, or thresholds after observing the score. Source-frozen reversal/alternative metrics, if later desired, must be separately labeled exploratory.

Add a known-ordinal synthetic positive and a no-ordinal control in tests, plus an independent auditor that reads neither the runner module nor its helper functions and recomputes all scores, ranks, shifts, hashes, and the frozen decision. CPU target under one minute, no paid API, compact result under 1 MiB. The two validation diagrams share leaf f73 and the four train diagrams share f72, so even a pass would need new independent imagery/text or a genuinely separate corpus witness before a claim about the manuscript's general code. Similarity alone is never a decipherment.
