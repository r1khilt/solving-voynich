# PATH-0002: locate explicit binding information across positions and layers

Registered 2026-09-22 UTC before any PATH-0002 model outputs. This is an adaptive but fresh-panel exploratory successor to PATH-0001's failed single-block attention-output control. The earlier JSPACE and NEURON verdicts remain failures; ROUTE-0001 remains exploratory. No historical manuscript labels enter this task.

## Question and method basis

PATH-0001's original-model attention patch at a single final query position was insufficient even when all 32 heads were replaced (3/16 full donor answers). ROUTE-0001 found strong position dependence on exposed country-codebook examples. We now ask where a newly provided two-slot binding can be causally changed: at the literal value-token positions, other earlier positions that may hold processed context, the final query position, or the entire prefix. The intervention is a complete post-block residual-state transplant at specified positions. It is broad and cannot alone identify a semantic feature. The mathematical possibility of earlier KV-mediated bypass and the original [QK/OV decomposition](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html) motivate the position comparison; [circuit-tracing methods](https://www.transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/methods.html) show why any later head-level explanation needs original-model causal validation. No validated Voynich application supplies ground truth for this method, so this is a known-answer synthetic qualification exercise.

## Fresh panel

Twelve new name/value bundles, disjoint in name and value vocabulary from PATH-0001, are fixed in `path2_tasks.py`. Three prompt templates each receive four bundles. Every bundle has both queried slots and both value orientations, giving 48 binding records, plus 24 unrelated literal-copy controls under the same swapped record fields. Source and donor differ only by swapping the two values; the question and required output form remain identical. Render all prompts through the frozen Qwen chat template. Verify source/donor equal token length, at least two differing token positions before the unchanged question suffix, and exact donor pairing before loading outcomes. Store rendered tokens only in ignored local output, with tracked hashes. The bundle is the independent sampling unit; reverse orientation and slot queries are dependent.

The same cached Qwen3-8B four-bit snapshot, dense-F32 block conversion and greedy 12-token output as the previous workspace studies are used. No new weights, training, paid API, or external data. Baseline gate: at least 24/48 binding records must be both-clean-correct with different first generated tokens, and at least 20/24 source copy controls must be correct. If the gate fails, return **inconclusive** without interpreting interventions. No task example or layer will be dropped after seeing intervention outcomes. Primary success denominators are both-clean-correct records; all-case and per-bundle rates are retained.

## Fixed intervention grid

At zero-based post-block layers 15, 19, 23, 27 and 31, capture the full prompt residual field for each clean source and donor. The primary layer is 23, chosen before PATH-0002 outcomes from the earlier routing diagnosis. For each record, compute the donor-minus-source field and apply one of seven fixed conditions to the source prompt: identity; only the final query position; only the differing record-value token positions; all earlier positions **excluding** those differing value tokens; all earlier positions; all prompt positions; and independent Gaussian directions at the differing value positions matched to each donor displacement's norm. The random seed is 51051 and depends deterministically on record ID, layer and position. There are 72 records × five layers × seven conditions = 2,520 generations, plus clean baselines. No parameter/head search or mid-run condition selection.

The complete all-position donor state is an exact positive control for the first generated token at each layer; check donor-logit equality within 0.002 and donor argmax equality on every record. Full greedy continuation may differ because lower-layer caches remain from the source, so report complete answer separately. Identity must reproduce the source baseline exactly. Native field execution must agree with independent per-position additive patches on generic inputs within 0.002 before scoring. Fail numerical gates rather than treating them as negative science.

## Registered decisions

The full-field control is adequate if at least 90% of eligible binding records produce the accepted donor answer and at least 95% of baseline-correct copy controls remain correct at primary layer 23. If this fails, both positional hypotheses are **uninformative**. Conditional on support, a literal-value-position route is **supported exploratorily** if its donor-answer rate is at least 50%, at least 20 percentage points above norm-matched random, and copy preservation at least 95%. An earlier-context bypass is **supported exploratorily** if the all-earlier donor rate is at least 50%, its advantage over final-only is at least 25 points, and copy preservation at least 95%. Neither criterion proves a unique route or necessity. Report all layers, prompt templates, bundles, and outputs regardless of verdict. Use a fixed seeded bundle bootstrap (10,000 resamples) as descriptive uncertainty, never to adjust thresholds or select a layer.

## Resource and stop rules

One model resident at a time, max 45GB MLX allocation and 50 minutes including model load and baselines. Stop on numerical/input failure or the cap, retain a clear incomplete record, and never silently retry with a changed panel. No manuscript final split is scored. Save compact intervention rows/metrics and source hashes; ignore full state arrays and token dumps. Before final claims, independently recompute correctness, denominators, condition grid, numerical gates, and decisions from saved outputs.
