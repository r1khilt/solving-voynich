# ANCHOR-0001 results: plant-image identity does not support simple lexical transfer

**Registered question.** Do five independently published pharmaceutical-fragment ↔ herbal-page visual matches place the fragment's `Lf` label unusually close in spelling to a `P0` word on the matched herbal page, compared with other `Lf` labels on the same pharmaceutical page? The assay and decision gate were frozen in [`ANCHOR-0001.md`](ANCHOR-0001.md) before lexical scoring, at source commit `e6bb251`.

**Sources and inputs.** The visual correspondences are Zandbergen's [Quire 15](https://www.voynich.nu/q15/index.html) and [Quire 19](https://www.voynich.nu/q19/index.html) assessments, not established botanical identities. Exact fragments, pages, label loci, and official Yale scan metadata are in `data/manifests/anchor0001_pairs.json` (SHA-256 `e7a6ece194c68bce287c6e203f2058cadf599a5d03857d768f005b6df0ec9806`). Pinned ZL3b train/validation corpus hashes are `49618c7be69cef573fe9ad8ae3627ad9f7b495601af1897aafb6082837fceca4` and `9bee4149f26fc49b49e4a826b73598dfb79a1e785731c15a1763489315a2efd3`; split manifest SHA-256 `9fc80cb4b000fdd5d952b7c2416b37b6b92f07bc56486a63309142953ed6634e`. No `test.jsonl` is read by the runner. The f70 test-text inspection accident during earlier triage is disclosed in NOTEBOOK NB-113. Four fragment labels have direct editorial number locators; fragment #54's label locus was inferred from layout order before scoring.

**Execution and checks.** Ran `PYTHONPATH=.:src .venv/bin/python -m voynich.anchor_transfer` once after the source freeze. It took 0.0245 seconds CPU and no paid API. Compact result: `results/ANCHOR-0001/results.json`, SHA-256 `5f3e717ec086f0927a24d2debf4fdda87aad023b8ac0529838bc25b29d5acf07`. The separate `scripts/anchor0001_audit.py` independently reconstructed all five input rows and scores, checked source/corpus/manifest hashes, enumerated all 1,440 within-page injective assignments, and reproduced the decision. The full pre-score repository suite passed 1,054 tests with 8 skipped and 23 subtests; focused tests, Ruff, and diff hygiene passed.

All five labels were eligible. The mean rank fraction was **0.6349** against an exact-assignment null mean of **0.5**; the one-sided exact probability was **0.3694**. Exact word recurrence was **0/5**. The registered support gate required mean ≥0.75, exact probability ≤0.05, and at least four of five ranks ≥0.5; the observed data miss all three. The preregistered decision is **`not_supported_in_this_small_panel`**. The four direct-locator pairs average 0.7381; this is a descriptive sensitivity result, not a second significance test.

**Interpretation.** The three train-side Quire 19 pairs rank fairly well, but the two validation-side Quire 15 pairs rank near the bottom. With only three pharmaceutical source pages, these five labels have low independent power and source-specific effects could dominate. More importantly, this test assumes that a fragment label should recur approximately as a word somewhere in the matched herbal paragraph. That can fail even if the drawing match is genuine: the paragraph may omit a name, the label may denote a plant part, morphology or abbreviation may intervene, or the transcription/label alignment may be wrong. The result rejects this *simple spelling-transfer prediction on this panel*, not visual reference or a historical decipherment theory.

Earlier [Stolfi label-match maps](https://www.ic.unicamp.br/en/~stolfi/EXPORT/00-EXPORT/97-10-23-label-maps/Welcome.html) already warn that many near label/text matches can arise by chance. The next meaningful attempt should assemble a larger, independently image-rated set before inspecting its labels, especially published small-plant ↔ small-plant matches where both sides carry labels. [Knowles' visual-match catalog and discussion](https://www.voynich.ninja/thread-4067-post-56211.html) is a candidate source, but its candidate quality and access must be checked first. Freeze image ratings, label alignment, and a new untouched evaluation group before choosing any richer string metric or decoder. No plaintext word, species name, language, or manuscript reading has been recovered.
