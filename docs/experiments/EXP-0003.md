# EXP-0003 — What context and components support Voynich prediction?

Registered 2026-09-20 PDT / 2026-09-21 UTC before context/causal outcomes. Exploratory, using the model family selected by EXP-0002's registered near-tie rule. Final manuscript test remains unscored.

## Rationale and prior methods

Voynich neural/saliency precedents and cipher limitations: [prior work](../research/PRIOR_WORK.md). Causal metrics/corruption choices can change conclusions: Zhang and Nanda, [Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042), ICLR 2024. Therefore retain absolute paired loss changes and multiple controls. We are testing what a trained predictor uses, not claiming a historical mechanism.

## Fixed procedure

- All three selected-family seeds: at most 8 eligible targets per validation page, uniform without replacement, seed 1201; exactly 128 preceding units, no page crossing, target absent from input. Exclude uncertain/unknown/EOS targets. Keep spaces/newlines. Save exact target coordinates. Some short pages have no eligible contexts.
- Same targets: full 128, last 64, last 16, last 4 units; shuffled distant 112 units with local 16 unchanged (seed 1202). No target-based or loss-based example selection.
- Report mean bits and paired damage against full128, plus equal-physical-leaf means and 2,000 cluster bootstrap replicates (seed 1203). Descriptive intervals with few clusters; no token-level independence claim or multiple-comparison significance claim. This denominator differs from full-validation model-selection loss.
- Seed 42 only, exploratory component localization: for every head, patch its clean projected contribution at the final query position into the remotely shuffled context. Compare a mismatched donor (batch rotation) and zero-ablation damage on clean input. Same example list; no favorable-example filtering. Mismatched donors can share a page and are an imperfect negative control.
- Identity patches and complete final-residual restoration must each have maximum logit error below 1e-5. Report absolute loss effects, avoiding normalized recovery when the corruption effect is tiny or negative. No attention picture is considered circuit evidence.
- Context shuffling/truncation and zeroing can be off-distribution. These tests neither distinguish semantics from style/copy/layout nor establish that a localized head implements a unique algorithm. Final-position effects are only part of a circuit.

Budget: three context profiles, one complete head sweep, local MPS, at most 10 minutes planned; no paid API, no further training, no adaptive model selection from these effects. Source, checkpoint and corpus digests retained. Stop on failed identity/provenance controls or nonfinite results.
