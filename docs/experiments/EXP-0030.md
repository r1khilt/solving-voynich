# EXP-0030 — corrected copy-mutate recovery calibration

**Registered before any EXP-0030 holdout score.** Corrective, exploratory synthetic study following [CHANNEL-ALIGNMENT-0001](CHANNEL-ALIGNMENT-0001-erratum.md). Not a Voynich reading, language identification, or a repaired version of the historical EXP-0023 result.

## Question and prior basis

After fixing the multi-character-null/mask mismatch, can the existing 70,301-parameter TinySignalModel plus the already-defined typed program catalog recover the **actual pre-null cipher stream** on held-out Finnish synthetic samples, including copy-mutate-only rows, above matched deletion controls? The original EXP-0023 score cannot answer this because its labels were misaligned. Do not infer that a model of an invented channel identifies the Voynich production process.

The previous Voynich-focused application is the EXP-0011→0014/0016/0023/0024/0026 sequence; its easy-filler controls and copy-containing erratum are explicitly separated in CHANNEL-ALIGNMENT-0001. [Graves et al. (2006)](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~graves/icml_2006.pdf) justify CTC for unsegmented sequence alignment; here the source target is directly known and the network is much smaller, so their results do not predict recovery. [Ravi and Knight (2011)](https://aclanthology.org/P11-1002.pdf) frame decipherment with language and channel models; our task has full synthetic supervision, unlike actual decipherment. [Hauer and Kondrak (2016)](https://aclanthology.org/Q16-1006.pdf) apply a different anagram/script hypothesis to Voynich; it does not supply evidence for inserted nulls. The fixed architecture and catalog are kept to isolate the generator correction, rather than launching another uncalibrated architecture search.

## Frozen evaluation and controls

1. Before interpreting any score, separately replay the generated holdout with a wrapper recording the true pre-null `C(L)` and verify **every** row's visible text/mask alignment and source subsequence. The usual stored `ciphered` target alone is not an independent oracle because it is derived from the mask. Zero failures required for a valid calibration.
2. Search the existing seven-operation typed catalog on WORLD_C validation only, at most 200 programs, with score `recon_acc − 0.02 × (n_ops−1)` and the existing null-feasibility gates. Compare selected score against the mean of 20 random same-length programs. Do not inspect Finnish results when choosing a program.
3. On Finnish holdout, report all-family and **copy-only** reconstruction, null recall/precision and predicted-null rate. Score 20-seed matched-random deletion for both populations, vocab-filter baseline, and frozen easy-filler EXP-0013 checkpoint + `exact_count_neural` no-retrain control. Keep the EXP-0014 frozen all-family reconstruction threshold **0.2043264147237504** for historical continuity, but do not numerically compare old malformed-copy scores as corrected-channel performance.

**PASS** only if the independent source-oracle validity check has zero failures and all existing EXP-0023 gates hold: all-family null recall/precision ≥0.50, predicted-null rate in [0.15,0.45], reconstruction >0.2043264147237504, vocab-filter mask F1 ≤0.55, selected validation score > random-program mean. In addition, at least **60 copy-mutate Finnish rows** must occur and copy-only reconstruction must exceed copy-only 20-seed matched-random reconstruction by **strictly more than 0.05**. **FAIL** otherwise; report all metrics and which condition failed. This is a calibration PASS/FAIL for the specified model/channel/holdout, not evidence of a Voynich null layer.
