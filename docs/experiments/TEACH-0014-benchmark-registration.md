## Resource gate and stop rules

The proposed campaign envelope is at most eight accelerator-hours, 12GiB MPS allocation and4GiB of combined result/checkpoint artifacts. The benchmark itself has a30-minute stop. The process MPS memory fraction is capped at the smaller of0.40 and `12GiB / torch.mps.recommended_max_memory()`, with sampled allocation checked every update. The benchmark records24 timings per arm, uses the median of the20 post-warmup steps, projects both seeds ×6,000 steps ×11 arms, multiplies that training projection by1.5 and adds30 minutes for evaluation and I/O. It passes only if the conservative projection is at most eight hours and the memory/artifact caps held. A failure stops before scientific training and requires an explicit prospective amendment; it does not license quietly dropping arms, shrinking steps after seeing confirmation, or calling a shorter run equivalent.

Before the benchmark, the trainer requires a clean committed version of all listed source, design, auditor and test files and records their SHA-256 values and Git HEAD. The result lives under ignored `results/TEACH-0014/benchmark.json`; no large checkpoints or loss archives are committed. The no-model artifact auditor independently recomputes timing medians/projection and verifies source and resource fields. The eventual scientific run additionally requires a passing source-matched benchmark, independently audited complete artifacts and sampled checkpoint-to-logit numerical replay. Parser edge and causal/J-space claims require separate diagnostics and competence gates.

## Known limits

The present visible two-operand grammar is exactly parseable by stripping markers/gaps and pairing ordinary symbols. Learned edge extraction can show whether a network acquires this public grammar; it cannot establish unknown Voynich grammar discovery. The TEACH-0014 evaluator remains a synthetic method qualification. A high score would not decipher any manuscript text, identify a historical language, or ground words to folio imagery. A low score would localize a model/task failure only to the extent that the controls and parser/reader interventions pass.
