# EXP-0036 — does word order carry transferable boundary information?

Registered 2026-09-25, before EXP-0036 manuscript scores. This is **exploratory** on the repeatedly exposed ZL3b validation split; the final test split remains untouched. Linked hypotheses: HYP-004 and HYP-005. A positive result is a constraint on candidate production models, not evidence for literal copying, a null layer, language, or meaning.

## Why this question

The corrected synthetic copy/null model in EXP-0032–0035 has not established a manuscript analogue. EXP-0028 already tested local one-edit *word* copying against a stronger spelling prior and within-locus shuffles, and did not support its practical gain; repeating that assay would not add evidence. [Parisel's 2026 positional analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19762v2) motivates looking at word-boundary dependence, but its plug-in mutual information and four-signature benchmark are descriptive and sensitive to transcription and sampling. [Timm and Schinner's self-citation materials](https://github.com/TorstenTimm/SelfCitationTextgenerator) motivate one possible explanation; [Grave et al.'s cache model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.04426) makes ordinary linguistic recency a rival. We test a *prospective predictive* boundary statistic with strict line-preserving controls; unlike Parisel, we do not infer writing direction or evaluate a four-signature historical mechanism.

## Frozen inputs and statistic

Read only `data/processed/zl3b/train.jsonl` SHA-256 `49618c7be69cef573fe9ad8ae3627ad9f7b495601af1897aafb6082837fceca4` and `validation.jsonl` SHA-256 `9bee4149f26fc49b49e4a826b73598dfb79a1e785731c15a1763489315a2efd3`. These are ZL3b 2025-05-13 EVA-codepoint transcriptions; `data/manifests/zl3b_source.json` and `zl3b_split.json` record source and physical-leaf split. No test text, final holdout, alternate transcription, image labels or model checkpoint is accessed. This assay does **not** treat EVA codepoints as proven historical glyphs.

Within each locus, split the text on whitespace and retain maximal contiguous runs of words matching `[a-z']+`; any uncertain/rare/segmentation token interrupts a run. Do not form pairs across loci or pages. Each adjacent pair contributes `(last codepoint of earlier word, first codepoint of later word)` over fixed alphabet `a–z` plus apostrophe. Train a Laplace-smoothed conditional table `P(first_next | last_prev)` with additive count 1 on **train only**; compare against its identically smoothed unconditional next-initial distribution. The per-pair log2 ratio is a held-out predictive gain in bits. No parameter is selected using validation. Report micro mean, per-leaf means, section slices and pair counts.

Primary contrast: observed validation gain minus the mean gain across **200** independent within-locus word-order permutations, seed `360036 + replicate`. The permutations retain each run's multiset, length, locus, page, section, transcription exclusions and most layout, but destroy its actual order. Use a **2,000-draw physical-leaf bootstrap** seed `360136` of the paired contrast; all validation leaves receive equal sampling probability, with pair-weighted aggregation within a draw. The exploratory `boundary_order_signal` label requires contrast at least **0.02 bits/pair**, bootstrap lower bound >0, observed gain above the 95th percentile of the 200 permutation gains, and a positive-control gain contrast at least **0.1 bits/pair**. Otherwise label `not_supported` if the positive control passes, or `invalid_positive_control` if it does not. No after-result threshold changes.

Validity controls: a fixed toy eight-symbol generator creates training/evaluation word sequences with `P(next initial = prior final)=0.8`, versus otherwise uniform initial symbols; a separately shuffled-training conditional table on the real data is reported as a negative diagnostic. Independent auditor must recompute train counts from raw processed pages, all observed/permuted pair gains, 2,000 bootstrap draws, toy control and decision from saved seeds rather than trusting the runner's summary. Hard CPU cap 180 seconds, no paid API or external download. Source/code/registration must be committed and pushed before the first manuscript score. Keep compact JSON results and hashes in Git; record exact command, runtime, failures and exclusions in NOTEBOOK. A positive signal would be compatible with language, stateful cipher, layout-conditioned formulae and copy processes; it does not choose among them.
