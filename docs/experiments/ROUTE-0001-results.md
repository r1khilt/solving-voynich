# ROUTE-0001: intervention location changes the conclusion

**Both prespecified exploratory diagnostics supported at block24.** Earlier-context donor replacement redirected15/16 eligible answers versus0/16 for final-position replacement. Extending the restricted country swap to all prompt positions redirected5/16, versus0/16 for final-position swaps and matched random fields. Copy preservation was12/12 in every condition. These results diagnose the failed local method; they do not revise either completed primary verdict or establish a general workspace.

## Exact scope and denominators

The panel contained48 anchored-alias development records and12 new copying controls. All paired rendered prompts had equal length. The unedited model answered31/48 semantic records correctly; only16 records had **both** clean source and donor correct, the registered diagnostic denominator. Those16 come from just two unordered country pairs: Germany/China12 and Germany/Brazil4. France/Brazil supplies zero eligible records under this criterion. Reverse directions, paraphrases and shared facts remain dependent. This is especially far from16 independent replications.

The chosen layers and criteria were frozen before this successor's outcomes, but were motivated by the already exposed main study. No final examples were reused. The registration calls this exploratory, not a new confirmation.

## Mechanistic interpretation

At block24, earlier positions are sufficient for15/16 donor answers while the final position is sufficient for none. Four blocks later, final-position donor information is sufficient for12/16, and earlier-position information for4/16. This is consistent with a transfer of task-relevant information toward the final query position as computation proceeds. It directly shows why treating the final position as the only relevant site was inadequate in the earlier test. It does **not** identify the particular attention heads or prove a unique country variable.

The within-example intervention combinations add detail. At block24,15 donor cases succeed with earlier or complete replacement; one requires combined coverage. For country swaps, three cases succeed with earlier and complete coverage, two only with complete coverage, and eleven with neither. These are descriptive response patterns, not a new threshold or independent evidence set.

The country-coordinate method remains limited: block24's five successes are2/4 eligible capital cases and3/6 language cases, with0/4 continent and0/2 currency cases. More positional coverage improves this restricted method without making it reliable across functions. We have not established necessity, a sparse complete circuit, or transfer to unknown scripts.
