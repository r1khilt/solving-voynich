# TEACH-0013: causal mechanism of raw serialized two-hop binding

**Prospective preregistration, written before reading any live TEACH-0012 outcome, final report,
checkpoint, prediction, activation, loss curve or confirmation score.** The design reconciles the
prospective raw-mechanism, circuit-development and token/causal-geometry programs with the frozen
TEACH-0012 task and the project research protocol. It is conditional on TEACH-0012 qualification;
it is not a result and its choices must not be revised after TEACH-0012 outcomes become visible.

## Entry condition and restricted claim

TEACH-0013 may begin only if the independent TEACH-0012 audit validates the campaign and both
`raw_deep` seeds receive the exact preregistered `RAW-SEQUENCE-BINDING-QUALIFIED` label. The
`parsed_memory` positive control and `raw_null` leakage control must also pass their registered
health criteria. This entry rule is conjunctive. A near-pass, one-seed pass, diagnostic aggregate,
or favorable checkpoint does not qualify.

If the entry condition fails, record `TEACH-0013 NOT ENTERED` and do not screen trained raw states
under this registration. A narrower study may be registered later, but it cannot inherit the
confirmatory language or thresholds here.

The primary question is whether a discovery-frozen state or dynamic path in a qualified raw causal
Transformer carries an episode-specific intermediate `k = F(n)` that is both necessary and
sufficient for recipient-specific `G(k)` retrieval under new logical families, positions,
renderings and distractors. Secondary questions ask whether the causal representation separates
key content from relation binding and physical order, which attention/MLP route writes and reads
it, and how the final mechanism develops across saved training checkpoints.

A pass establishes a causal abstraction in this synthetic generator only. It does not identify a
Voynich token, plaintext, language, cipher, word boundary, semantic category or historical
mechanism. Attention, probe accuracy, cosine similarity, CKA, RSA or a J-lens label alone cannot
support the primary claim.

## Frozen models and analysis scope

The primary models are the two final 8,000-update `raw_deep` checkpoints from TEACH-0012, with all
weights and inference settings frozen. No retraining or fine-tuning is allowed. The final
`raw_shallow` checkpoint may be reported as an architecture diagnostic only if it independently
passes every absolute raw binding gate. The final `raw_looped` checkpoint may enter an explicitly
secondary cross-architecture analysis only if it independently passes every absolute gate and the
registered `LOOPING-COMPETITIVE` label. Neither can rescue failure of the two primary seeds.

The raw-deep residual stream has cuts `0..12`: cut 0 is the token embedding and cut `l` is after
complete transformer block `l`. The architecture has no learned absolute position embedding;
rotary position information enters Q/K inside attention. Attention and MLP sublayer
inputs/outputs are retained for later decomposition, but the first discovery screen uses only
complete residual states to limit multiplicity.

## Fresh grouped generator and immutable split

Use the frozen TEACH-0012 vocabulary, oracle, logical grammar and rendering rules without changing
model input semantics. Use a new namespace `TEACH-0013-v1`. Generate from seed `73111`; assign a
group by the leading 64 bits of the SHA-256 digest of its complete logical specification modulo
10, with buckets 0--4 discovery and 5--9 confirmation. Continue generation until each split has
exactly 128 accepted groups. Group assignment occurs before any model inference. All variants of
one logical group remain in the same split.

Every group contains:

1. one queried name `n` and two first-table assignments `F0(n)=k0` and `F1(n)=k1`, with `k0 != k1`;
2. three recipient second tables `G0`, `G1`, `G2` with independently permuted mappings and distinct
   `Gj(k0)` and `Gj(k1)` within each table;
3. donor targets `G0(k1)` that differ from `G1(k1)` and `G2(k1)`, so a fixed donor answer is
   distinguishable from a reusable key on changed recipients;
4. four signal chains plus two false paths that terminate at signal objects and two independent
   distractor chains;
5. paired marked and fully marker-free renderings;
6. two complete row-order permutations with the same logical graph;
7. a same-key/different-format rendering and a same-key/different-distractor rendering;
8. matched first-hop, direct, composed and copy tasks on the same logical symbol inventory.

For each base program, construct five causal donor families while matching sequence length, row
count, `GAP` pattern, delimiter multiset, answer/query positions and distractor topology wherever
the logical intervention permits:

- **F-content donor:** change only the queried F value from `k0` to `k1`.
- **G-content donor:** preserve `F(n)=k0` and change only `G(k0)`.
- **binding donor:** preserve the complete symbol multiset and physical row positions while
  swapping the right sides of two F edges or two G edges.
- **order donor:** preserve every logical edge but move complete rows to different physical slots.
- **format/distractor donor:** preserve the signal computation while independently changing edge
  markers, gaps or irrelevant chains.

The generator rejects a group unless the symbolic oracle verifies every answer; all row functions
are single-valued; the required answer distinctions hold; no sequence exceeds 128 tokens; and the
model-visible token sequence plus task/query has only one registered oracle label. The manifest
stores logical edges, every token occurrence, render ID, semantic labels, expected outputs and
rejection counts. An independent parser/auditor regenerates the oracle from saved tokens without
calling the training generator.

## Semantic positions

Physical token indices vary, so every occurrence receives a generator-derived semantic label used
only by analysis. Repeated role classes are deterministically subindexed by canonical logical-row
ID, and markers/gaps are subindexed by physical order, so the labels exhaust every model-visible
position rather than pooling several occurrences:

- queried-F left and right endpoints;
- matched-G left and right endpoints for `k0` and `k1`;
- other signal F/G left and right endpoints;
- false-path shared and terminal endpoints;
- independent-distractor endpoints;
- edge markers and gaps adjacent to each row;
- task, query and final `ANSWER` positions.

Repeated symbols remain separate occurrences. A semantic site is `(residual cut, semantic label)`,
not a token identity or absolute index. Semantic labels are never provided to the raw model.
Discovery and confirmation use identical label code and reject missing or multiply assigned
required roles.

## Stage A: clean eligibility on the fresh panel

Before causal selection, score both final raw-deep checkpoints on all discovery clean variants.
Generate and hash the confirmation manifest, but do not run confirmation inference until the
complete discovery decision object is frozen. After that freeze, run the clean confirmation panel
first; continue to causal confirmation only if it passes the gates below.

Each seed must achieve on confirmation:

- at least 90% composed item accuracy across all base/donor recipients;
- at least 80% exact three-recipient groups for both F assignments;
- at least 85% exact marked/marker-free pairs;
- at least 85% exact two-order pairs;
- at least 85% exact same-logic/different-distractor pairs;
- at least 95% first-hop and direct accuracy and at least 98% copy accuracy.

These are assay-eligibility gates in addition to TEACH-0012 qualification. If either seed fails,
the final mechanism verdict is `INCONCLUSIVE: FRESH-PANEL COMPETENCE`; site maps may be retained as
exploratory diagnostics but no mediator, subspace or circuit support label may be issued.

Primary denominators always contain the full 128 groups. A both-clean-correct subset is reported
separately and can diagnose intervention mechanics, but it cannot replace the full denominator or
select examples.

## Stage B: full residual site discovery

At every residual cut `0..12` and every semantic position above, patch one donor occurrence into
the matched base occurrence. Screen F-content, G-content, binding, order and format/distractor
donors separately on all 128 discovery groups and both model seeds. Report exact target answers,
target-probability change, base preservation, fixed-donor answers, legal-candidate membership and
all wrong destinations.

For F-content sites, the target is the recipient-specific `Gj(k1)`. A discovery site qualifies
only if, in both seeds, it reaches at least 75% donor-target items, 60% exact three-recipient
groups, 90% changed-recipient non-injection, and a mean donor-target probability increase of at
least 0.35. It must reach these thresholds separately on marked, marker-free, first-half-position
and second-half-position strata. The strata are fixed from generator metadata.

Select the **earliest** qualifying residual cut. At that cut choose the semantic position with the
largest minimum exact-group rate across seeds and the four strata; ties use minimum item accuracy,
then smaller fixed semantic-label index. Freeze this as the primary key site. If no single site
qualifies, screen ordered two-site paths only: an earlier relation-endpoint write followed by a
later query or `ANSWER`-position write. Candidate paths are ordered by earlier cut, later cut,
source semantic-label index and destination index. Patch the earlier donor write, recompute to the
later cut, then patch or retain the propagated later write according to the saved path protocol.
Select the earliest path meeting the same thresholds. No paths with more than two sites are
eligible in the primary registration.

If neither a single site nor an ordered two-site path qualifies, freeze `NO STATIC/TWO-SITE
MEDIATOR` before opening confirmation. Later head analysis may describe dynamic routing but cannot
produce the primary key-mediator label.

G-content, binding, order and format screens do not choose the primary key site. Their full maps
are saved, and their strongest discovery sites are selected by the same deterministic rule for
secondary confirmation. This prevents post hoc selection of whatever variable looks clearest.

## Stage C: frozen recipient-specific interchange

At the selected site or path, compute one donor under `(F1,G0,n)` and reuse that unchanged donor
for bases `(F0,G0,n)`, `(F0,G1,n)` and `(F0,G2,n)`. Repeat in the reverse `F0 -> F1` direction.
Run the following frozen confirmation conditions:

- clean base and donor;
- identity patch and manual native-state recomputation;
- F-content sufficiency in both directions;
- reverse necessity, inserting the matched base-key state into the donor-key computation;
- corruption followed by native-state rescue;
- same-key/different-format, order and distractor patches;
- input-level queried-F replacement as a task positive control;
- complete final preclassifier donor state as a fixed-answer injection positive control;
- norm-matched Gaussian delta, seed `73211`;
- cyclic next-group donor, norm-rescaled;
- wrong-position donor from the nearest physical token with a different semantic label;
- wrong-key donor from the next group whose intermediate differs;
- direct and copy specificity controls.

`RECIPIENT-KEY-TRANSFER-SUPPORTED` requires the fresh-panel competence gate, a discovery-qualified
site/path, numerical qualification, and all of the following in both seeds on the full confirmation
panel:

- clean base, donor and input-level F replacement at least 95%;
- F-content sufficiency at least 85% recipient-target items and 70% exact three-recipient groups in
  each patch direction;
- changed-recipient non-injection at least 95%, where non-injection means output is not the fixed
  donor answer `G0(k1)` or its reverse-direction analogue;
- reverse necessity at least 80% restored-base items and 65% exact groups;
- rescue at least 95% of cases whose registered corruption changed the clean prediction;
- same-key format, order and distractor preservation at least 95%;
- true-patch item accuracy at least 40 percentage points above every Gaussian, cyclic,
  wrong-position and wrong-key control;
- mean finite target-probability increase at least 0.40 over clean base;
- direct and copy accuracy loss no greater than five percentage points under same-key nuisance
  patches;
- final-state answer-injection positive control at least 95% fixed donor answers.

Sufficiency without necessity is labeled `SUFFICIENT PARALLEL ROUTE`, not complete mediation.
Necessity without sufficiency is `NECESSARY BUT NOT TRANSPORTABLE`. Repeated fixed donor answers are
`ANSWER INJECTION`. One-seed support is `NOT REPLICATED`.

## Stage D: content, binding and order geometry

Use discovery groups only to construct balanced factorial contrasts in which key content,
relation membership, physical row order, absolute token position, format and distractor topology
are crossed. At the frozen primary cut and semantic position/path endpoints, fit three candidate
orthogonal subspaces:

1. **content:** contrasts among intermediate keys after averaging over binding, order and format;
2. **binding:** same-edge versus different-edge contrasts after averaging over content and order;
3. **order:** physical occurrence/row-order contrasts with logical edges and content fixed.

Remove the discovery mean, estimate contrast covariance, and take eigenvectors in descending
eigenvalue order. Candidate ranks are fixed to `1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64`; select the smallest rank
whose discovery finite patch reaches 95% of that variable's full-state target-probability effect
and passes the variable-specific exact threshold. Orthogonalize in the fixed order content,
binding, order; report the unorthogonalized overlap and repeat a diagnostic reverse order so basis
dependence is visible. No gradient-optimized rotation on confirmation is allowed.

Confirmation tests content alone, binding alone, order alone, content+binding, all three, the
orthogonal complement, 32 Haar-random matched-rank subspaces, shuffled-label contrasts and
equal-energy off-subspace ablations. It also reports rank, participation ratio, explained
contrast covariance, principal angles and discovery-fitted cross-seed Procrustes transfer.

`CONTENT-SUBSPACE-SUPPORTED` requires, in both seeds:

- content-only recipient transfer at least 80% items and 60% exact groups;
- at least 90% changed-recipient non-injection;
- at least a 35-point item advantage over the best random or shuffled control;
- content-complement preservation of the original base answer at least 90% and donor-target rate
  at most 10%;
- reverse necessity or equal-energy content ablation reduces clean composed accuracy by at least
  30 points, with native restoration at least 95%;
- direct first-hop key readout remains consistent with the same content edit while copy changes by
  no more than five points.

`BINDING-SUBSPACE-SUPPORTED` requires binding-only re-pairing to produce the oracle result on at
least 75% items and 55% exact groups, a 35-point advantage over order-only, shuffled and random
controls, and at least 90% content preservation on matched direct/first-hop readouts.

`ORDER-SHORTCUT-SUPPORTED` is a diagnostic label when the order component changes outputs according
to physical occurrence on at least 75% of adversarial re-renderings while the binding component
fails its gate. If order-only effects stay at or below 20% and logical binding survives
re-rendering, report `ORDER COMPONENT NOT CAUSALLY SUFFICIENT`; do not infer that order information
is absent.

Static embedding neighbors, raw cosine, centered/whitened cosine, CKA, RSA, linear probes and
task-conditioned Jacobian/J-lens frames are secondary diagnostics. Any J-lens frame uses
discovery-averaged Jacobians only and must pass the same finite recipient-transfer controls before
being called causal.

## Stage E: dynamic head and path decomposition

Only after the primary site/path and subspace ranks are frozen, decompose the actual route. At
each transformer block between the earliest selected writer and latest selected reader:

1. patch each attention head result and the MLP result separately on discovery;
2. screen all nonempty head subsets within one block, selecting the smallest subset that reaches
   90% of the full attention-result finite effect in both seeds;
3. decompose selected heads with the full base/donor Q/K/V factorial at dynamically labeled query
   and source occurrences;
4. sum per-source-token contributions through the output projection and verify reconstruction;
5. screen ordered writer-to-reader edges with path patching while leaving off-path components at
   clean base values;
6. test selected path, its complement, all attention, MLP-only, all residual and complete final
   state.

Subset and path selection maximizes the minimum recipient-target group rate across both seeds;
ties use fewer heads/edges, earlier layers and lexicographic indices. Confirmation uses only the
frozen masks. Attention mass is reported by semantic source label but cannot satisfy a gate.

`DYNAMIC-PATH-SUPPORTED` requires, in both seeds:

- selected heads/path at least 80% recipient-target items and 60% exact groups;
- at least 90% changed-recipient non-injection;
- selected path at least 35 points above equal-size random heads/paths, cyclic donors and
  wrong-semantic-source paths;
- path exclusion reduces full-state transfer by at least 30 points;
- adding the path to the registered corrupted baseline restores at least 80% of the full-state
  target-probability gain;
- complement-only donor transfer at most 20%;
- marker-rich discovery to marker-free confirmation and marker-free discovery to marker-rich
  confirmation each retain at least 70% item transfer;
- all exact reconstruction and endpoint identity checks pass.

Q/K/V receives a descriptive label (`Q-only`, `K-only`, `V-only`, a combination, or unresolved)
only when the corresponding cell reaches 75% items, 55% groups and 90% non-injection in both
seeds. Multiple cells may qualify; no unique attribution is forced.

## Stage F: checkpoint-development analysis

Developmental analysis starts only after the final-checkpoint site/path, component masks,
subspaces, alignment method and thresholds have been frozen and confirmed. Apply that exact
explanatory object backward to raw-deep checkpoints at updates `250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000,
8000`; never rediscover a best site, head, path or rank at each checkpoint.

Use one additional fixed 128-group checkpoint panel from seed `73311`, separate from site
discovery and final confirmation. It crosses first-hop/direct/copy/composed tasks, marked and
marker-free renderings, two row orders, 0/2/4/6 distractors, factorial swaps, endpoint-sharing
false paths, heuristic-conflict layouts and recipient triples. All checkpoints see the same
groups.

For each checkpoint and seed report:

- exact behavior, target probability, entropy, legal-candidate rate and heuristic destinations;
- final-frozen content, binding and order readout/subspace measures;
- final-frozen recipient transfer, necessity, rescue and matched controls;
- selected-head/path sufficiency, exclusion and complement effects;
- head-result norms, selected QK/OV products and effective ranks;
- CKA/RSA to the final checkpoint and discovery-fitted Procrustes/principal angles;
- finite intervention effects versus local Jacobian predictions;
- item transitions between adjacent checkpoints with logical-family bootstrap intervals.

Because the TEACH-0012 curriculum changes at updates 2,000 and 4,000 and checkpoints are sparse,
this analysis may describe a developmental sequence or bound a change to an interval. It may not
claim an optimizer-intrinsic phase transition, a precise critical update, or causal attribution to
a training-data family. An effect appearing after 2,000 or 4,000 is reported as occurring after a
curriculum change with cause unresolved.

Cross-checkpoint state transfer is secondary. Fit orthogonal Procrustes maps on checkpoint-panel
discovery halves only and test the held-out halves; include shuffled-example, shuffled-checkpoint,
random-orthogonal and equal-rank CCA controls. Bidirectional finite recipient transfer supports a
stable functional code; geometric alignment without transferred behavior does not.

## Numerical qualification

Before scoring scientific gates, require in every seed and relevant condition:

- identity residual replacement reproduces native logits with maximum absolute error below
  `1e-6`;
- manual attention-head reconstruction and summed source contributions differ from native head
  results by less than `1e-6`;
- the full projected source delta matches the complete attention delta below `1e-6`;
- selected-path all-component reconstruction matches the corresponding full intervention below
  `1e-6`;
- probabilities are finite and normalized within `1e-6`;
- untouched positions and off-path components match the registered base or donor source exactly
  as specified.

A failed numerical or identity gate makes the affected analysis `INCONCLUSIVE`. Preserve the
artifacts and register any precision-only repair before rerunning. Do not loosen the threshold or
alter scientific selection based on exposed aggregates.

## Resource ceilings and stop rules

Before checkpoint inference, benchmark 32 discovery groups across all 13 residual cuts and
semantic positions. Project the complete campaign conservatively at 1.5 times measured runtime.
Proceed only if the projection is at most four accelerator-hours and 300 GB of transient
activation traffic. Hard ceilings are four accelerator-hours, 24 GiB sampled MPS allocation,
20 GiB ignored local artifacts and 2 GiB tracked compact artifacts. Stage B has a two-hour
sub-ceiling; Stages C--E together have a 90-minute sub-ceiling; checkpoint analysis has a
90-minute sub-ceiling.

Stop at a ceiling, nonfinite tensor, source/checkpoint hash mismatch, suite-hash mismatch,
semantic-label ambiguity or numerical failure. Report `INCOMPLETE` for the affected stage; do not
silently reduce groups, positions, layers, heads or controls. No training, model download,
network service, paid API, manuscript data or manuscript final-test scoring is permitted.

Before inference, commit and hash the registration, source, environment lock, command, frozen
TEACH-0012 checkpoints and prediction manifests. The launch guard rejects dirty registered files.
Retain SHA-256 hashes for:

- generator and independent oracle/auditor source;
- complete discovery, confirmation and checkpoint-panel manifests;
- logical groups, token sequences, render IDs and semantic-position maps;
- both final and milestone checkpoints;
- every selected and rejected site, path, head mask and subspace definition;
- per-item logits, predictions and finite intervention rows for all primary and control cells;
- numerical errors, benchmark/runtime/resource logs and RNG states;
- discovery decision object frozen before confirmation access;
- final report and independent audit.

The discovery decision object records all candidates, deterministic tie breaks, selected site or
path, component masks, subspace bases/ranks, alignment maps, thresholds and suite hashes. Hash it

split isolation and sequence uniqueness, reproduces every selection and decision from compact

Report both seeds separately, exact grouped denominators and family-cluster bootstrap intervals.
Retain negative and failed candidates so multiple comparisons remain visible. Do not choose a
cleaner seed, format, checkpoint or semantic role after confirmation.

## Frozen verdicts and failure interpretations

The primary verdict is `RAW-KEY-MECHANISM-SUPPORTED` only if
`RECIPIENT-KEY-TRANSFER-SUPPORTED` and `DYNAMIC-PATH-SUPPORTED` both pass in both raw-deep seeds,
with numerical qualification and independent audit. `CONTENT-SUBSPACE-SUPPORTED` strengthens the
description but is not required when the causal algorithm is demonstrably dynamic and lacks one
compact static code. Binding and order labels are secondary and independently gated.

- Fresh competence failure: the new assay cannot test a general mechanism; no mediation claim.
- No residual or two-site mediator: static bottleneck unsupported under this search; broader
  dynamic computation remains possible.
- Recipient transfer repeats `G0(k1)`: answer transport, not a reusable intermediate.
- Sufficiency passes but necessity/path exclusion fails: a sufficient parallel route, not complete
  mediation.
- Probe/subspace decodes but finite patches fail: accessible correlate, not causal code.
- Binding edits follow physical order: ordering shortcut under the tested renderings.
- Random, cyclic or wrong-position controls approach the selected effect: specificity unsupported.
- Marker-rich and marker-free circuits fail cross-format transfer despite behavioral invariance:
  redundant format-specific implementations, not one universal parser.
- Final mechanism is absent at early checkpoints: record its bounded developmental appearance;
  do not call the interval a sharp phase transition.
- One seed passes: not replicated.
- Numerical, positive-control, hash or independent-audit failure: inconclusive or invalid as
  specified, never scientific failure.

Passing all clauses would show that two independently initialized raw synthetic models implement
a discovery-frozen, recipient-sensitive causal route for two-hop binding and would characterize
how that route changes over the stored curriculum. It would remain a calibrated synthetic result,
not decipherment.
