# EXP-0004 — Can interpretation recover known hidden state and filler?

Registered 2026-09-20 PDT / 2026-09-21 UTC before synthetic generation/training/probe outcomes. This is a deliberately small calibration, not a historical Voynich model and not a decipherment attempt. It addresses HYP-001/HYP-002/HYP-003 provisionally; see the hypothesis register for their actual scope.

## Prior work and scope

The [prior-work review](../research/PRIOR_WORK.md) covers synthetic decipherment and competing Voynich generators. [Singh et al., ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/singh24c.html) use controlled synthetic data and causal activation manipulation to study induction formation. This motivates controlled calibration; we are not reproducing their experiment or identifying induction heads here. [Adriaensen and Maene, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06045v2) extract Moore-machine abstractions of transformers trained on regular languages. That supports investigating compact state, but does not establish that our generator or Voynich is such a language; no automaton extraction is implemented in this experiment. [Zhang and Nanda](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042) motivate explicit patching controls. These are primary-source methodological precedents, not evidence that the manuscript contains filler. The additional primary records were accessed 2026-09-20 PDT; the new readings cover their abstracts/bibliographic summaries, with no full replication or claimed audit of all paper details.

## Generator and identifiability

- Four hidden states, eight symbols `abcdefgh`, two symbols per state. Initial state uniform. With probability 0.65, advance state by one modulo four and emit either corresponding symbol uniformly. Otherwise leave state fixed and emit any of the eight symbols uniformly. The latter events are null/filler relative to state progression.
- Signal and filler have identical marginal symbol distributions under the stationary uniform state prior. Roles are not visually marked. A filler can coincide with the expected next-state symbol; perfect role identification is impossible. The true Bayesian filter over four states supplies posterior state/role estimates and next-symbol probabilities. It knows the true rules; the neural model does not.
- Negative control: IID uniform symbols, independent uniform state labels and independent Bernoulli(0.65) role labels. Hidden labels are unrecoverable from observed text. This detects spurious probing success.
- Independently generated pages: 600 train, 96 validation, 192 synthetic test, length 255 symbols each, generator seed 2201 with separate SeedSequence children per split, assert no duplicate full pages. Fixed alphabet and rules across splits. No disjoint-key/language/mechanism-family transfer claim; those are future calibration requirements.
- Labels are stored in synthetic metadata for later scoring; the language-model trainer consumes only visible text, BOS/EOS and next-unit targets. Synthetic models never load Voynich weights or text, and their results never score the manuscript test set.

## Training and diagnostic probes

Each family uses the same `small` architecture (2 layers, width 128, 4 heads, FF 352, context 256) and optimizer settings, seeds 42/43/44, at most 1,000 updates, eval every 100, patience 8. At most 6,000 local updates, no paid APIs. Select checkpoints only on synthetic validation. Preserve configuration, manifests and digests. Planned budget 15 minutes on MPS, abort on integrity/nonfinite failures.

After fitting the language model on text alone, fit **supervised diagnostic readouts** to synthetic training labels. This is explicit supervision in the interpretation stage, not an unsupervised cipher solver. Fixed ridge least-squares one-hot readouts (alpha 1, train-standardized features), no tuning on test. Positions: 24 evenly spaced integer character indices from 32 through 223 inclusive on every page (14,400 training and 4,608 test positions). Compare observed-symbol embedding with final residual features for four-way latent-state and binary signal/filler prediction. Report accuracy, balanced accuracy/class recall, training-majority, full Bayesian oracle and last-four-symbol oracle baselines. Also report next-character prediction bits at these positions versus uniform 3 bits and Bayesian reference. No test-selected retraining.

Primary calibration question: does the final residual improve over the embedding baseline on the structured generator while the IID control remains near chance? Report all seeds and both tasks, including failure. No hard significance claim from densely sampled correlated positions.

## Causal state steering, seed 42 only

In structured synthetic test pages, use each first-128-symbol prefix. Select pairs by generator-filter information only: same last observed symbol, different posterior most-likely states, both confidence at least 0.6. First qualifying donor per recipient, at most 64 pairs in page order; report pair count and donor reuse, and report inability if no pair qualifies.

Take the top three left singular directions of the centered four-way state probe's weights in raw residual coordinates. At the final residual position, replace only the recipient's projection onto these directions with the donor's projection. Measure KL distance in bits from the resulting next-letter distribution to the donor's Bayesian next-letter distribution (condition on emitting one of eight letters). Compare unchanged recipient, full residual donor replacement, and a random orthonormal rank-three subspace (seed 2207) with the same per-example activation displacement norm. Identity and full-donor reproduction tolerance 1e-5. Save individual effects, not only favorable cases. Single random subspace and reused donors limit strength; this is causal calibration of a supervised direction, not a unique minimal circuit.

Promising performance would justify harder generators and label-free discovery methods. It would not establish any Voynich filler, homophony, plaintext, language or semantic decoding.
