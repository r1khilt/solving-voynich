**Pre-packet registration, 2026-09-25.** Neither the 92 selected review crops nor their image gap measurements have been generated or inspected. The question is whether the editorial certain (`.`) and uncertain (`,`) separators differ in **direct manuscript ink** on the eight fresh training-assigned folios whose page layouts passed BOUNDARY-CHANNEL-0005. A pass would support a physical segmentation signal under this estimator; it would not identify plaintext word boundaries, a language, or a cipher. A fail is useful: it tells us that training a decoder on the current separator labels is not yet physically justified.

## Prior evidence and method choice

[Reddy and Knight (2011)](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511/) documented end-to-start dependence in Voynichese, which makes segmentation relevant but does not identify words. [Rozanova and Temerev (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17096v1) distinguish editorial marks, human word-box annotation and direct ink; their six-folio pixel pilot is the exact measurement algorithm reused here. Our BOUNDARY-CHANNEL-0001 found box-gap AUC `.895` versus direct-ink `.616` on accepted pilot rows; 0002 reproduced the published 300 pixel records exactly; 0003 found suspect vertical locators on two of those six folios. The new 0005 image-only layout search passed its registered batch identity gate on **eight different train physical leaves**. It still cannot certify individual word boxes. This experiment therefore places a label-blind crop check in front of any separator conclusion and compares the image measurement to a deliberately mislocalized image control. These are not independent manuscripts, and the method, labels and threshold ideas were developed after previous pilots; this is prospective only with respect to these selected crop/gap outcomes.

## Frozen decision and controls

First run `PYTHONPATH=.:src .venv/bin/python scripts/boundary_ink_0006.py --mode packet`. Review every `B000`…`B091` crop **without opening the key or using manuscript text/gap measurements**. A `pass` means both colored source boxes visibly cover the intended two adjacent ink groups with an inter-box split between them. Mark `fail` for an empty box, art, wrong line, gross horizontal offset or a split inside a glyph; mark `unsure` otherwise. Treat `unsure` as nonpass. Record all 92 decisions as a blind-ID-to-`pass`/`fail`/`unsure` JSON, commit and push it **before** opening the key or scoring ink gaps. This same-agent image review is label-blind but not an independent paleographic adjudication, and the displaced controls can sometimes land on genuine neighboring writing.

The **visual QC gate** requires at least **65/76** originals pass, at most **4/16** displaced controls pass, and at least **three passing originals on every folio**. If it fails, report all descriptive results but make no physical-separator claim. If it passes, run `PYTHONPATH=.:src .venv/bin/python scripts/boundary_ink_0006.py --mode score` exactly once. For each visually passed original, reuse the published local threshold/component/split estimator at the 0005 image-only affine, retain only algorithmic `qc=ok` finite gaps, and divide gap pixels by the mean transformed height of the two source boxes. AUC asks whether a `.` gap exceeds a `,` gap; half credit for ties. Report same-row box-gap AUC only as a proxy comparison. Run the estimator on all 1,024 pairs as an **unreviewed exploratory** population, never as the primary claim.

The **physical-effect gate** requires the visual QC gate, at least **30 certain and 20 uncertain** reviewed/algorithmically accepted pairs, direct-ink AUC **≥.65**, the lower endpoint of a **2,000-draw folio-block bootstrap 95% interval >.50**, and a **≥.05** AUC advantage over a `+20`-pixel horizontal mislocalization on the subset where both locations have finite `qc=ok` measurements. Report paired baseline and shifted support, all per-folio counts, missingness and failures. This is deliberately more demanding than the exposed six-folio direct-pixel AUC `.616`. No threshold or selected folio may be amended after seeing packet/gap results; an amendment would be exploratory and a further untouched sample would be needed for a new prospective claim.

Expected cost: four ignored 1,280×696 contact sheets and 92 crops, under five minutes of local image rendering; one pass over 1,024 local pairs plus 76 shift checks, likely under three minutes CPU; no paid API or model training. Pinned source and selection must be committed, pushed, and remote-verified before generating the packet. Raw scans/crops, key and full measured rows stay ignored; compact metrics, blind ratings and notebook observations are tracked. A positive result still does **not** validate a word-by-word transcription or enable a translation. Named-glyph identity and external semantic anchors remain separate requirements.
