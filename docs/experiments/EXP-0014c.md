2. Clip \(p_i\) to \([10^{-6}, 1-10^{-6}]\).
3. Dynamic program over position \(\times\) keep-count \(\times\) last-state \(\in \{\mathrm{keep}, \mathrm{delete}\}\) maximizing
   \(\sum_i \log p_i\) (if keep) or \(\log(1-p_i)\) (if delete), plus transition logs taken from the **already-written** EXP-0012/0013 classical sticky path: stay-keep \(0.88\), keep→delete \(0.12\), stay-delete \(0.72\), delete→keep \(0.28\).
