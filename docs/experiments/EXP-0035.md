# EXP-0035 — uncertainty-aware training for source-free copy-channel recovery

**Prospective source/language holdout and fixed objective comparison.** This is synthetic mechanism calibration, not a Voynich decipherment. Registration and code must be committed/pushed before training; validation-only checkpoint selection must be frozen and pushed before the first Portuguese score.

## Question and prior basis

EXP-0032's 10.746M-parameter source-free canonical model learned some copy-null localization but failed practical recovery on Polish. EXP-0033 showed every copy example had multiple string-compatible masks; EXP-0034 showed a very strong *full-source* generator posterior localized nulls well, but that oracle cannot be used at inference. Does replacing the **arbitrarily sampled training mask** with a posterior distribution over masks that produce the same known training source improve a source-free model on a *new* language and book? Hard-label training is unbiased in expectation; the proposed benefit is variance reduction and removal of contradictory supervision in a finite-data/finite-training setting, not a theorem of greater asymptotic expressivity. [Graves et al. (2006)](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~graves/icml_2006.pdf) motivate summing over latent sequence alignments but standard CTC collapses repeated labels, unlike our keep-subsequence channel. [Neural String Edit Distance (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08388) and [Proper losses for learning from partial labels (2012)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2012/hash/217eedd1ba8c592db97d0dbe54c7adfc-Abstract.html) supply related transduction/soft-target principles, not evidence of success on Voynich. Prior Voynich/cipher applications and caveats: `docs/research/deep-review-2026-09-21/DECIPHERMENT.md`, CHANNEL-ALIGNMENT-0001, EXP-0024, EXP-0030–0034 and `docs/research/PROTOCOL.md`.

## Matched models, resources and decisions

Use the existing 6-layer, width384, 8-head, 10,746,629-parameter canonical-symbol Transformer and *the same* 12,000 rows, fixed batch-selection RNG, model seeds320101/320202, batch22 WORLD_C +10 other, AdamW3e-4, dropout0.1, 3,000 steps, evaluation every250 steps and validation-copy hard-mask null-F1 checkpoint selection as EXP-0032. Two new objectives × two seeds: uniform-target and generator-weighted-target. For soft keep probability `q`, the mask loss is the expected **same class-weighted BCE** used in the hard run, `q·softplus(-logit)+2(1-q)·softplus(logit)`; world loss0.10×cross-entropy unchanged. No Portuguese or iid rows are loaded by training. Existing **hard-mask canonical** seed-matched EXP-0032 checkpoints are the fixed baseline; freeze file SHA-256 `69a8000b6f7310a9a330fbf3fa89d614fd71004794c928e6a874a8668ea07fbb`. Probe MPS memory/time before four fits; hard cap600s per fit and300s evaluation, no paid services. Do not launch if the measured four-run estimate materially exceeds one hour without a new bounded plan.

On the Portuguese copy rows use the same fixed top90/128 keep decoder for all six models. Report null precision/recall/F1, length-penalized reconstruction, full-mask exact rate, and 20-seed matched-count random; report periodic positive and iid negative controls. Primary contrast is the two-seed paired **weighted-soft minus hard** copy null-F1 (micro per seed averaged), 2,000 shared-row bootstrap draws seed350350. Register a practical objective benefit only if positive controls all ≥0.70, all iid scores ≤matched random+0.05, mean primary F1 gain≥0.03 with lower95% bound>0.01, and weighted-soft mean copy reconstruction exceeds hard by≥0.03. Otherwise, valid controls with failed benefit gates mean **NOT_SUPPORTED**; failed controls mean **INCONCLUSIVE_OR_INVALID**. Secondary uniform-soft minus hard and weighted-soft minus uniform contrasts are descriptive. No threshold or checkpoint may change after Portuguese scoring. A separate auditor must recompute all saved masks/family metrics/bootstraps and replay direct model probability probes before interpretation.

The comparison is an objective study, not proof of a unique decipherment algorithm. A synthetic PASS only motivates a separately registered manuscript test against no-null, random deletion and structured nonsemantic controls on untouched folios; it does not identify the Voynich language, null rate or any word.
