# EXP-0005 — Distant order, symbol mixture, or page category?

Registered 2026-09-20 PDT / 2026-09-21 UTC before these results. Exploratory follow-up to EXP-0003, linked to HYP-003 and M-002. Manuscript final test remains unscored. No new neural-model fitting.

## Background and competing explanations

[Reddy and Knight 2011](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511.pdf), sections 2.3 and 6–7 (read 2026-09-20 PDT), report vocabulary partitions, local concentration and page-level variation. Their HMM/Currier results do not make a cluster semantic. Our prior [review](../research/PRIOR_WORK.md) covers earlier Voynich neural, cipher and nonsemantic accounts. [Grave, Joulin and Usunier](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.04426) adapt language predictions to recent history through a neural cache. Our simpler count adjustment is independently specified below, not a replication of their architecture.

EXP-0003 suggests older context could help through frequency, page category or ordered dependencies. Hold local context and total length fixed to distinguish these explanations more carefully. A positive category effect would not identify a topic, language or cipher.

## Fixed design

- Existing compact Voynich models, seeds 42/43/44, frozen best checkpoints from EXP-0002. Same registered train/validation corpus and tokenizer.
- Up to 32 eligible targets per validation page, sampling seed 5201, exactly 128 previous units, no target/future in context. Same exclusions as EXP-0003. This larger sample can overlap earlier exploratory validation and is not independent final confirmation.
- Conditions: original128; last16; shuffle original distant112; replace distant112 with training-only windows from same versus different illustration category, either random or nearest by symbol histogram; ordered and shuffled versions of every replacement. Always preserve original last16. Categories are the existing `I` illustration metadata, not inferred semantics or Currier labels.
- Donor windows: width112, stride32, entirely within training pages, excluding BOS/EOS. For each same/different group, sample up to128 candidates without replacement; first is the random donor, minimum histogram total-variation distance is the matched donor. Matching uses preceding inputs only. Record donor page/start and distances; it is approximate matching, not exact frequency equality.
- Four corruption repetitions, seeds5202–5205; average losses across repetitions per target before aggregation. Score in batches64 using MPS, at most10 minutes planned. No paid API, no neural weight updates.
- Primary contrasts: truncation damage; original histogram-preserving shuffle damage; different-minus-same category loss before/after approximate histogram matching; shuffle-minus-ordered for each replacement type. Report individual seeds and sample/equal-leaf means with 2,000 descriptive leaf bootstrap draws (seed5206). Few physical groups and multiple comparisons preclude strong significance or historical conclusions.
- Additional fixed predictors: training-only five-gram; histogram-reweighted five-gram with exponents0.5 and1, local distribution `(count_remote+32*p_global)/(112+32)`, multiply five-gram probabilities by `(p_local/p_global)^exponent`, then normalize; 50/50 global/category five-gram mixture, explicitly metadata-assisted. No tuning on validation/test.

## Decision and failure boundaries

Report every contrast, including contrary seeds; do not declare a historical hypothesis confirmed from a small loss difference. If distant histogram/order controls account for most benefit, prioritize adaptive-frequency mechanisms; if matched category replacements retain a clear gap, investigate higher-order category differences. If effects vary across seeds or matching distances remain unequal, retain the ambiguity. No unstable percentage-recovery metric when a denominator is tiny.

Verify preserved suffixes, donor split/category constraints, nearest-match distance no worse than the sampled random candidate, order-invariance/normalization of histogram baselines and frozen checkpoint/corpus identities. Abort on failed controls or nonfinite scores. Retain source and input/output provenance, all per-target scores, and compact summaries.
