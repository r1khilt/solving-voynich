# EXP-0029: held-out locus association beyond word shape and page vocabulary

Registered 2026-09-24 before any EXP-0029 model score. This is **adaptive exploratory manuscript research** on already-exposed ZL3b validation pages, not plaintext recovery or independent confirmation. Final manuscript test stays unscored.

## Question and source basis

EXP-0028 found that most exact-repeat cache benefit survives shuffling words *within* each transcription locus. Can a relationship between different words, learned only from training leaves, identify which word belongs in a held-out locus after frequency, surface form, page topic and locus type are controlled? A positive answer would support studying a reusable locus-level predictive state. It would **not** establish semantic meaning or distinguish grammar, topic, scribal formula and encoding.

[Montemurro and Zanette (2013)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0066344) already analyze Voynich word distributions and cosine similarity over manuscript partitions, interpreting networks as potentially semantic. Their full-corpus networks and shuffled-word significance are not this masked-locus, leaf-held-out discrimination test. [Reddy and Knight (2011)](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511/) recovered broad Currier divisions with an HMM; rediscovering section labels is insufficient. [Gutmann and Hyvärinen's contrastive-estimation work](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/gutmann10a.html) motivates evaluation against an explicit negative distribution; our paired logistic score is a limited discriminative predictor, **not** a normalized language model or an NCE decipherment. Published [word-similarity evaluation cautions](https://aclanthology.org/W16-2506/) motivate frequency/form matching and a held-out behavioral endpoint instead of treating cosine as semantics.

## Corpus, examples and isolation

Use pinned ZL3b train/validation derived SHAs `49618c7be69cef573fe9ad8ae3627ad9f7b495601af1897aafb6082837fceca4` / `9bee4149f26fc49b49e4a826b73598dfb79a1e785731c15a1763489315a2efd3`. Train-fitting uses the same deterministic65 physical-leaf groups as EXP-0027/28;16 other train leaves fit the pairwise readout. Refit the word prior and locus association counts on all81 train leaves only after readout fitting. Validation comprises10 physical leaves; no test text read. Restrict primary analysis to `P0` transcription loci and certain words matching `[a-z']+`; a locus is not automatically a physical line.

For each `P0` locus with at least4 eligible words, take each target occurrence that appears exactly once in its locus and at least3 times in training. Mask that target; the remaining eligible words are the context. Candidate negative is a word from a **different `P0` locus on the same page**, absent from the target locus, with training count≥3, exactly equal string length, training-count ratio in [0.5,2], sharing the first **or** last character with the target, and having the *same minimum Levenshtein distance to the target locus context*. Choose one eligible negative by the smallest SHA-256 of `(seed=290029,page,locus_id,target_slot,negative_word)` among sorted candidates. If none exists, skip and count the reason. This matching makes page/section, frequency, length and a principal form-to-context similarity uninformative by construction; other form differences remain and are modeled. Feasibility inspection before outcomes found approximately770 fit/calibration pairs and ~974 validation pairs under the65-leaf count table; exact realized counts will be recorded. Multiple pairs from one leaf are correlated, so uncertainty uses physical leaves.

## Predictor and controls

Fit a baseline paired preference model with three candidate features: `log P_static(word)` from EXP-0028's fixed beta1,000,000 / gamma0.1 global spelling prior; mean normalized Levenshtein similarity to context; and maximum normalized shared-prefix-plus-suffix length to a context word. Compute target-minus-negative feature differences. Standardize each dimension by its RMS on training-calibration pairs (no mean subtraction); fit a no-intercept logistic readout with L2 coefficient0.01 by deterministic Newton steps. Report the probability that the target rather than the matched negative belongs to the locus, bits/pair log loss and accuracy with half credit for exact ties.

The full model adds one feature: a shrinkage co-occurrence score. In fit leaves, count once per `P0` locus whether each word and each pair of distinct words occurs. For candidate `w` and context word `x`, estimate `P(x|w)=(C(w,x)+20 P(x))/(C(w)+20)` with `P(x)=(C(x)+0.5)/(L+1)` over `L` training `P0` loci, and average `log[P(x|w)/P(x)]` over unique eligible context words. This is a pseudo-likelihood feature, not a joint generative law. Fit the four-feature readout on exactly the same calibration pairs and same fixed regularization; no validation-tuned parameters. The principal quantity is paired **baseline bits minus full-model bits** on validation. Preserve per-pair margins and per-leaf sums; bootstrap10 physical leaves2,000 times (seed290129) for its95% interval.

Null: for100 deterministic replicates (seed290229+r), shuffle eligible words across `P0` locus slots **within each training page**, preserving page word multiset, locus lengths/types and every noneligible event. Rebuild co-occurrence counts on the shuffled fit leaves, fit its four-feature readout on the same calibration pairs, refit co-occurrence on all shuffled train leaves, then score the fixed validation pairs. Keep the baseline unchanged. This tests whether learned within-locus associations beat page-topic composition; retain all100 null gains. A small synthetic topic-locus positive control in unit tests must show the association count score distinguishes known shared-topic pairs from matched cross-topic pairs; it validates direction only, not historical identifiability.

Exploratory `locus_association_support` requires real full-over-baseline gain≥0.02 bits/pair, physical-leaf bootstrap95% lower bound>0, real gain above the 95th percentile of the100 shuffled-training null gains, and full-model accuracy≥0.55. Otherwise `no_support_under_registered_controls`. Report all gates and negative outcomes without retuning. A pass would justify a subsequent independently grounded / held-out relation test; it would **not** label embeddings as semantic or permit a manuscript translation.
