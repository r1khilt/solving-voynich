## Why this model and what prior work supports

The project previously applied Apple Vision revision 2 to known historical herbals: the nine-image HERBAL-CONTROL-0001 fixed gate failed; the selected six-class HERBAL-CONTROL-0002 closed-set gate passed, but a strong pairwise one-to-one baseline outperformed the three-way assignment. These are applications to related source art, not Voynich decoding. [Kaoua et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08109) studied matching illustrations across medieval manuscripts and found strong global-feature retrieval in some settings alongside severe style and caption/depiction problems; the title labels alone are therefore insufficient ground truth. The [DINOv2 model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md) describes an image-only self-supervised ViT backbone and nearest-neighbor uses. [Registers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16588) address high-norm background patch artifacts. The published results at general-vision scale do **not** establish performance on 216 selected historical chapter drawings, their metadata, unknown rejection, or Voynich art.

An exploratory probe, chosen after HERBAL-CONTROL-0002's Apple result was known, loaded the fixed DINOv2-with-registers base checkpoint and scored that panel's same 18 crop PNGs. It got raw nearest-neighbor **29/36**, three-way assignment **36/36** (six complete triplets), and pairwise one-to-one **36/36**. A separate manual preprocessing/inference replay matched all 324 saved distances within `1e-5` (maximum absolute difference `9.97e-8`) and independently re-enumerated the raw and pairwise scores. This is model triage on exposed, hand-selected data, not a pre-registered success, population estimate, cycle-specific effect or botanical proof. The exact code/result are `scripts/herbal_control_0002_dino_probe.py` and `results/HERBAL-CONTROL-0002/dinov2_exploratory.json`.

A subsequent post-result metadata diagnostic found that the six old classes have **identical folio-side order in all three manuscripts**. Aligning equal ranks, with crop x-coordinate only for BnF's shared-page tie, achieves **36/36 without image pixels**; see `results/HERBAL-CONTROL-0002/folio_rank_exploratory.json`. This makes DINO's old-panel 36/36 less distinctive as evidence of visual value, although it does not mean DINO itself uses folio metadata. The larger disjoint-class panel's pre-registered folio-only challenger is essential to interpret any new visual result.
