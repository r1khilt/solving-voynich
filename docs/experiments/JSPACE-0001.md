# JSPACE-0001: restricted Jacobian lens and shared-country interventions

Registered 2026-09-22 UTC before task scoring or corpus calibration. Numerical engineering pilots precede this registration and are explicitly exploratory. The user's fresh-start instruction supersedes the earlier stop after the ranked experiment block. No claim about the manuscript is tested here.

## Question and motivation

Does a corpus-averaged, vocabulary-linked derivative identify an internal country representation that can redirect multiple factual operations in a capable pretrained model, while preserving an unrelated copying task? This qualifies an interpretation method on known facts before attempting unknown historical semantics. A direct landmark-to-answer association can solve these prompts; baseline accuracy alone cannot establish an intermediate country variable.

Prior evidence and architectural rationale are in [METHODS_REVIEW](../research/latent-mechanisms-2026-09-21/METHODS_REVIEW.md), [FAILURE_DIAGNOSIS](../research/latent-mechanisms-2026-09-21/FAILURE_DIAGNOSIS.md), and [TASK_DESIGN](../research/latent-mechanisms-2026-09-21/TASK_DESIGN.md). WMD-0001 did not recover a semantic system; its mostly boundary/keep loss and privileged-oracle success motivate qualifying model competence first. EXP-0006 motivates the raw-output-direction comparator. The source method is [Gurnee et al.](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html) and its [official implementation](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens). This is a restricted dictionary adaptation on quantized Qwen, not a full replication of Claude J-space.

## Frozen input and representation choices

- Existing local `mlx-community/Qwen3-8B-4bit` snapshot `545dc4251c05440727734bcd94334791f6ab0192`, Apache-2.0; 36 blocks, width 4096, vocabulary 151936. No weight training, paid API, upload, or fresh model download. The exact numerical precision policy is qualified and frozen in the run manifest before scientific scoring.
- WikiText-2 training text from the PyTorch examples distribution, CC-BY-SA 3.0/GFDL Wikipedia-derived text. Preserve `<unk>` and tokenization artifacts; this is not the raw WikiText version. Split at top-level article headings. Seeded shuffle of eligible articles, one randomly located contiguous 128-token window per article. Require 512 distinct eligible articles; refuse silent reuse. Hash raw corpus, token windows, model files, software, task records, and relevant source files. No calibration-task prompts or task labels enter lens estimation. Facts may occur naturally in pretraining/calibration; independence is from this evaluation construction, not guaranteed factual disjointness.
- Source blocks `[3,7,11,15,19,23,27,31,34]`, zero based, post-block; target block 35 pre-final-RMSNorm. Skip the first 16 and final positions. Compute exact selected rows of the mean Jacobian: sum cotangents over valid target positions, average over valid source positions, average equally over prompts. Retain prompt derivative squares and independent article halves to diagnose cancellation and instability.
- Thirty-two token directions: bare and leading-space forms of the 16 words in `configs/jspace0001.json`. `mountain` was excluded solely because the bare form tokenized into two tokens in the engineering pilot; `tree` replaces it before task scoring. Require each form to be exactly one token. Fold final RMSNorm gamma into the unembedding rows. Average the two *unnormalized transported rows* per country, then normalize that mean for interventions; no selection of best token variant on final outcomes.
- This dictionary is undercomplete and selected around the task. It cannot establish all-vocabulary sparse J-space, consciousness, natural-language semantics in a glyph model, or an unsupervised historical decipherment. Greedy nonnegative decomposition of these 32 directions is descriptive only.

## Tasks, competence, and final isolation

Freeze task generator v1 with seed 51021 and record hash `11a61cf1833ec2c69c19670ce6096fff286867b720879ffb555bef5a24e83e42`: pilot 80, development 120, final 240. Pair combinations and template families are split; concepts and facts are shared. Repeated prompts and reverse directions are dependent. Use Qwen's one-user-message chat template with `enable_thinking=False` and assistant generation suffix. Only the `prompt` field is input; all labels stay in the evaluator.

Greedy generation, maximum 12 new tokens, stop at model EOS. Primary correctness is accent/case/whitespace normalization after removing only outer periods, exclamation marks, quotation marks, and backticks. Also retain literal-normalized exactness as a stricter diagnostic. No post-hoc synonym additions. Truncated outputs are retained and scored as produced, with a flag.

Capability gate on pilot: at least 80% correct in each semantic family (indirect facts and anchored aliases), at least 95% copy accuracy, at least three of four relations at 75% across semantic families. If it fails, stop the causal confirmation; diagnosis or a new teacher is a separately labeled successor. Calibration does not rescue incompetence.

## Causal intervention and controls

At each source block, read the last rendered prompt position. For source/donor country directions D with normalized rows, set delta = D.T (D D.T)^-1 (reverse(Dh)-Dh). This exactly exchanges the two coordinates in the nonorthogonal frame with the minimum Euclidean displacement. The orthogonal complement is preserved. Reject condition number above 1e8; report errors, never silently drop failures. Strength is 1. Reapply this same fixed prefix edit during full causal recomputation of generated tokens; no generated position is independently edited.

Development evaluates all nine layers and six conditions: coordinate swap; same-norm random; same-norm random orthogonal to the two directions; raw unembedding/gamma coordinate swap; full naturally occurring matched donor residual replacement; zero/identity intervention. Random directions have deterministic independent record/layer/condition seeds. Donor is a broad positive control, not evidence of a selective representation. Clean recipient and donor answers are retained. Copy targets must remain unchanged and are excluded from desired-change denominators.

Select **one layer** by development swap success minus max(random, orthogonal, raw-swap success), on all semantic records whose clean recipient answer is correct. Break ties by the earlier layer. Require development copy preservation at least 95%; if no layer qualifies, report failure and do not optimize a new setting inside this experiment. Also report all-record denominators and donor-correct subsets. No strength or position selection in this primary experiment. Further position/dose/neuron analyses are exploratory and cannot replace this primary test.

After freezing a selection artifact tied to the source/config/lens hashes, score final once with all six conditions at that layer. Candidate support requires final swap success >=50%, advantage >=20 percentage points over each of random, orthogonal, and raw-swap, copy preservation >=95%, and >=30% swap success in **every** relation. These are operational criteria for this restricted task, not a theorem. Report all layerwise development curves, identity errors, absolute counts, full generated strings, delta norms relative to recipient/donor changes, and mean-Jacobian stability. Broad donor success with failed selective swap means the intervention site can affect answers but the proposed coordinates did not isolate the variable.

Only three unordered country pairs inhabit final: report per-pair results and leave-one-pair-out sensitivity, and explicitly decline strong population-level inference from a narrow clustered sample. Prompt-cluster bootstrap intervals are descriptive and do not create more independent countries.

## Validation, budget, and stopping

Before fitting: native selected-head forward agreement, additive identity equality, batched/single-row agreement, an analytic temporal toy, and central finite differences at multiple perturbation sizes. Quantized arithmetic can be noisy; preserve failed numerical pilots and improve precision before freezing. Require finite arrays throughout. Any material qualification failure blocks scientific conclusions.

Local Apple M5 Pro, 64 GB RAM. Initial engineering timing: four directions/all nine layers/128 tokens approximately 2.55 seconds, peak MLX allocation 11.02 GB. 512 prompts x eight direction batches is about 2.9 hours at that measurement, plus task evaluation. Hard fit cap four hours; evaluation cap two hours; MLX memory cap 45 GB. These are upper bounds, not an instruction to consume time after results are complete. Checkpoint every eight articles. No paid spending. Record wall time, MLX peak memory (not whole-system memory), source revision, interruption, and exact completed sample count. An incomplete fit must be labeled incomplete, never silently treated as the registered 512-prompt fit.

Repository final manuscript holdout remains untouched. Save bulk activations/lenses locally under ignored `outputs/JSPACE-0001`; track compact provenance, results, plots, and this record. No successful natural-language intervention by itself licenses a Voynich translation.

## Precision qualification frozen before scoring

The engineering pilots found finite-difference noise even with nominal float32 arrays. [MLX's numerical precision documentation](https://ml-explore.github.io/mlx/build/html/usage/precision.html) identifies reduced-precision matrix operations as a default on supported hardware. Final policy: `MLX_ENABLE_TF32=0` before backend initialization, expand the cached quantized values into dense float32 matrices in all seven projections of each transformer block, keep embedding/output head quantized, and use float32 residuals. This does not restore original unquantized pretrained weights.

Qualification passed: native/identity/future-to-past logit errors exactly zero, analytic temporal-map error 7.63e-7, batch relative derivative error 8.27e-5. Across three source layers and three finite-difference step sizes, maximum relative discrepancy 0.009923; each layer has at least two checks below 2%. The thresholds were fixed in the qualification runner before its scored execution. Measured batch runtime 2.289 seconds and peak MLX allocation 33.96 GB; the 512-prompt estimate is now about 2.60 hours for derivative calls, plus overhead and evaluation. Earlier failed precision pilots are retained as engineering diagnostics, not scientific outcomes. Source/direction batching error is small but nonzero, not exact-real-arithmetic equality.
