**Interpretation.** EXP-0036's signal cannot be explained *solely* by moving line-initial and line-final words: those are now fixed, and the train-fitted prediction still depends on local order after coarse interior position is preserved. This is a useful observable constraint for any future generator or cipher model. It is **not evidence that words were copied**: a language can create word-to-word dependencies, and finer position, topic, section, scribe and transcription conventions remain live alternatives. The assay uses one EVA-codepoint representation and a validation split repeatedly used for development; it is not independent confirmation. EXP-0028's failure of a specific one-edit local copy predictor is consistent with this result: a boundary dependency need not be an edited copy. A historically meaningful successor needs alternate grapheme/space interpretations, stronger position/section-aware no-copy baselines, competing meaningful and nonsemantic generators, and frozen evaluation on fresh physical leaves. No Voynich nulls, language, glyph meanings or plaintext follow from this result.
