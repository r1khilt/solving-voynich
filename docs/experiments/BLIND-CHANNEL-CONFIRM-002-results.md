## What is and is not localized

**Dictionary coverage dominates this test.** Keys7/13 have zero positive-weight
bank keys that literally emit the true whole tuple. Their best fitted mappings
match only2/23 and5/23generating rows; these are broadly wrong dictionaries,
not merely an unseen rare-letter assignment. A reader restricted to those
banks cannot output the exact correct text. This does not prove that all its
other candidates have the observed edit counts as a lower bound.

**Source/decision errors dominate the covered cases.** All14other true tuples
have positive bank support. Thirteen are nonexact and the current finite-bank
objective prefers a wrong tuple; one, key2, is exactly true. All14have separated
floating ranked-list bounds, so enlargingk cannot improve their current
finite-bank objective within the declared tolerance. That is not a global
key-search or real-arithmetic numerical proof. Key7's bound is unseparated;
key13's is separated despite catastrophic error and absent truth support.

**The unresolved next distinction is the fitting objective versus its search.**
The old order1stage scores16restart trajectories and passes one selected
mapping into order3 and large-source local refinement. Direct fitting-score
comparison with the now-exposed generating dictionaries and rescoring saved
restart endpoints can determine whether known better basins were discarded.
It cannot retroactively repair this confirmation or establish a global optimum.
A separate [DIAG-A registration](BLIND-CHANNEL-CONFIRM-002-DIAG-A.md) freezes that
bounded, all16case diagnosis before its one run. No new fitting or transfer
reading is performed by the diagnosis.

Neural source/proposal work remains worthwhile for the smaller within-bank
