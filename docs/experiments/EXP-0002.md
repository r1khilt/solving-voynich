## Registered design

- Frozen ZL3b source, section/leaf split v2, 112-unit training-only tokenizer and metrics unchanged from EXP-0001.
- Six configs: `small`, `reference`, `mtp`, `qk_norm`, `gated_attention`, `attention_only`.
- Seeds: 42, 43, 44. At most **2,000 optimizer steps per run**, eval every 100 steps, patience 8, existing batch/context/optimizer defaults. At most 36,000 optimizer steps total. Keep initialization/best/last snapshots and checkpoints at 100, 500, 1,000 and 2,000 if reached; these support later learning-dynamics analysis.
- Primary score: each seed's validation-selected best bits/token; rank configurations by mean across three seeds. Report standard deviation, individual values, selected steps, token exposure and stopping reason. These three optimization seeds are not three independent manuscripts.
- Baseline comparison unchanged: fixed five-gram etc. No test scoring, language claims, or dozens of adaptive parameter choices.
- Tie/near-tie rule: if mean difference from the simplest competitive candidate is under 0.02 bits/token, prefer the smaller candidate for later interpretation; describe this as an engineering threshold, not significance.
- Report learning curves and evaluate selected checkpoints on the training split as an overfitting diagnostic. Do not use those train metrics to rerank validation candidates.
