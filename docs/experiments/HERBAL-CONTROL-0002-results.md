# HERBAL-CONTROL-0002: fresh-panel gate passes, cycle-specific benefit unproven

On 18 drawings from six new historical herbal chapters and three manuscripts, the **registered closed-set visual-feasibility gate passed**. Raw directed nearest-neighbor retrieval got **17/36** chapter matches; the fixed, unlabeled three-way assignment got **28/36**, with **4/6** wholly correct triplets and a conditional exact relabeling probability of **46/518,400 = 0.000088735**. This is evidence that the panel's image distances plus a one-to-one constraint contain chapter correspondence information. It is not a Voynich plant identification or a decipherment.

The crucial limitation emerged in a clearly **post-result exploratory control**: optimal *pairwise* one-to-one matching got **32/36**, outperforming the three-way 28/36. Thus the registered gain over raw nearest neighbor is not evidence that the three-way cycle constraint itself helps. It may principally repair many-to-one nearest-neighbor collapse. The experiment's registered pass is preserved, but a cycle-specific advantage is **not supported** on this panel.

## Exploratory comparison and claim boundary

After seeing the registered result, enumerated all `6! = 720` one-to-one assignments for each manuscript pair using the same raw distances, with no labels in the optimization. The exact pairwise minima scored BnF–Egerton **6/6**, BnF–Casanatense **4/6**, Egerton–Casanatense **6/6**; counting both directions gives **32/36**. These three independently selected bijections form only **4/6** consistent three-cycles. `scripts/herbal_control_0002_posthoc.py` and `results/HERBAL-CONTROL-0002/posthoc.json` preserve the formula, full pair maps and top three global assignments. This check was not pre-registered and cannot become a new pass/fail criterion for this panel. It shows that a fair one-to-one comparison removes the claimed *incremental* benefit of three-way matching here.

The 18 illustrations were hand-selected because chapter metadata across these manuscripts was available, and one reviewer placed their crops after seeing full pages. Writing and decoration remain in some crops. BnF *arthemisia* and *arthemisia tagantes* share a page. Casanatense *terbentina* depicts a barrel/resin scene rather than the trees in the other two manuscripts. The exact p-value conditions on this selected panel and its labels; it is not a population generalization result. Apple Vision's feature representation is opaque, and no out-of-set rejection was measured: any closed-set one-to-one assignment must assign a novel Voynich drawing somewhere. **Do not rank or name f35v with this method.** Its image has previously been viewed; its reserved transcription remains unopened, and no Voynich word was scored here.

A later **post-result exploratory folio-rank diagnostic** further narrows the visual interpretation. On the same frozen 18 crop records, sort the six anonymous crops in each manuscript by source folio side and, only for BnF's shared folio, left-to-right crop x-coordinate. Align equal ranks across manuscripts, without reading class names for prediction or image pixels at all. The six class orders are identical in BnF, Egerton and Casanatense; this metadata-only rule gets **36/36** directed matches, six in each ordered manuscript direction. `scripts/herbal_control_0002_folio_rank_diagnostic.py` (SHA-256 `488cceac36eea1624ca026adfb34fa2fe7416599a663473987f7d43766dc388e`) and `results/HERBAL-CONTROL-0002/folio_rank_exploratory.json` (SHA-256 `656b10a764f3bde996d0ebb55e97e17fe526ad297fd1b75284b4c38df97def1b`) preserve all 36 row decisions; a separate direct sort reproduced the three identical class orders. This check does not alter the registered Apple gate or imply the vision model literally reads folio numbers. It **does** show that this selected panel cannot demonstrate a practical advantage over chapter-order alignment. A later exploratory DINOv2 36/36 on these same crops likewise does not establish visual-specific value over this baseline. The larger fresh-panel study therefore includes a pre-registered development-fitted folio-only challenger.

The next informative visual test is a *fresh open-set* known-answer panel containing chapters absent from the gallery and matched distractors, with an explicit reject option. It should compare raw nearest neighbor, independent pairwise bijection and three-way assignment on equal terms, and require stable margins plus independent chapter-image review. Only a calibrated external anchor could then support a frozen text prediction; this result alone licenses none.
