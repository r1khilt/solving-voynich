# PATH-0010: factorial later-attention reversion in a direct lookup

Registered before any PATH-0010 model outcome. This is a **fresh-word, adaptive exploratory** successor to PATH-0008. PATH-0008 showed that one selected four-head group in block29 removed the donor first token in7/32, below its frozen criterion, while reverting whole final-query attention writes in blocks28–31 removed it20/32. The current study asks whether the four-block effect exceeds every one-block edit on new examples and where nonadditive intervention responses arise. Blocks28–31 were selected by the *prior program*; fresh lexical bundles do not erase that adaptivity. There is no manuscript or semantic claim.

## Prior methods and interpretation boundary

The [QK/OV transformer account](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html) explains why attention is the route from an earlier value-token residual edit to an unchanged final query position. [Path patching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05969) and [original-model circuit tracing](https://www.transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/methods.html) motivate controlled interventions followed by native recomputation. [Multiple-mediator interaction analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27510) shows that one-component patching can depend on the states of other mediators, making a complete small factorial grid preferable to greedy one-block attribution. The [functional-ANOVA identifiability account](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/lengerich20a.html) is a caution: the subset contrasts here are **anchored finite differences over these sixteen interventions**, not a distribution-purified functional ANOVA or unique attribution of biological-style modules. Prior Voynich co-occurrence/embedding results reviewed in PATH-0004 and `SIMILARITY_THEORY.md` provide no validated translation or reason to transfer English-table circuits to the manuscript.

## Frozen panel and intervention grid

`path10_tasks.py` freezes eight new lexical bundles disjoint in names, value words and copy words from all PATH-0006 and PATH-0008 panels. Each bundle has two value orientations, two queried names, and an unrelated copy task per orientation, using the exact PATH-0006 direct-lookup renderer (four bundles per template):32 binding and16 copy records. Source/donor prompts differ only by swapping the two value fields. The local Qwen3-8B-4bit tokenizer must give equal source/donor prompt lengths, at least two changed positions before the common query suffix, and distinct expected source/donor answer words. Greedy generation is capped at12 tokens. A binding is eligible only if both clean full answers are correct and generated first tokens differ. Baseline competence requires≥24/32 eligible bindings and≥14/16 correct source-copy tasks; otherwise stop **inconclusive**. The lexical bundle, not its four orientation/query variants, is the independent resampling unit.

At zero-based post-block23, transplant the changed value-position residual field from donor into source. Let the four later final-query attention-write blocks be \(W=\{28,29,30,31\}\). For each task run **all sixteen subsets** \(S\subseteq W\), including the empty upstream-only edit and the full four-block clean-source reversion. At every selected block replace the final-query attention write with its clean-source write and recompute every subsequent original-model block; no static logit subtraction. Also run one seeded Gaussian write edit at all four blocks, norm-matched **per block to clean-minus-uncut-edited writes** captured on the upstream-only run, and the architectural clean-write cut across all blocks24–35. This random control matches the uncut reference norms, not the conditional norm after earlier random edits; it is not a semantic null. Seed510111. No per-subset tuning or selection from these new bundles.

## Metrics, factorial contrasts and frozen decision

For each eligible binding where the upstream-only edit gives the **full donor answer**, record the donor-minus-source first-token logit margin \(m(S)\), donor-first-token removal, full source/donor answer, and copy preservation for every subset. Define the reversion effect \(R(S)=m(\varnothing)-m(S)\), so positive values reduce donor preference. Report all16 per-subset rates and margins, including failures and any nonmonotonicity. The primary **full-versus-any-one-block** continuous contrast per record is \(D=\min_{i\in W}\{m(\{i\})-m(W)\}=R(W)-\max_i R(\{i\})\). Compute an equal-weight mean of the eight bundle means of \(D\), not a prompt-weighted mean. Also report the full-window first-token removal rate minus the **largest of four** prespecified single-block rates on the same eligible upstream-success denominator. That maximum is a fixed comparator, not a posthoc winning block claim.

For every nonempty subset \(T\), calculate anchored Möbius interactions \(I(T)=\sum_{U\subseteq T}(-1)^{|T|-|U|}R(U)\). Report single-block effects, all six pair interactions, four triple interactions, the four-way term, and the full effect versus sum of four singles. These are signed *intervention-response* contrasts under nonlinear downstream recomputation. Neither a nonzero term nor a nonmonotone dose identifies a unique circuit or physical communication path. Use10,000 seed-fixed lexical-bundle resamples for descriptive intervals on the equal-bundle mean \(D\) and the full-minus-best-single rate difference; do not treat variants as independent observations or infer a p-value from these adaptive experiments.

The factorial-window claim is **exploratory supported** only if: clean competence passes; the upstream value edit gives full donor answers on≥75% of eligible bindings; whole-prefix donor transport, zero edit, and all-later source first-token logit controls each pass max absolute error<0.002 and matching argmax; at least90% of baseline-correct copy answers survive both upstream and full-window interventions; the full window removes the donor first token on≥35% of upstream successes and beats the norm-matched random full-window edit by≥20 percentage points; its removal rate exceeds **each** single-block rate by≥20 percentage points; and the equal-bundle mean \(D\) is≥2.0 logits with positive bundle mean in at least6/8 bundles. If the upstream or numerical gate fails, report uninformative. If the full-window positive control (<35%) fails despite valid upstream, report **uninformative positive-control failure**. Otherwise failing any primary condition is a qualified exploratory negative. Thresholds and denominators do not change after outcomes.

## Bounds and verification

There are48 tasks ×(16 subsets + random full-window + all-later cut)=**864 generated intervention records**, plus clean baselines and bounded numerical checks. PATH-0006 took229 seconds for432 multi-block interventions and PATH-0008 took1,007 seconds including3,968 head-screen prefills; a roughly8–20 minute PATH-0010 runtime is a planning estimate, not a measurement. One-shot cap **30 minutes elapsed /45GB peak MLX allocation**, with a recorded partial failure if exceeded. No training, new model/download, paid API, manuscript final-test score, or automatic rerun. Freeze seed, model snapshot, task/rendered-token/source hashes and command; keep large arrays/token IDs under ignored `outputs/PATH-0010`, compact complete rows, controls, summary, decision and independent audit under `results/PATH-0010`. Tiny-Qwen numerical tests must validate subset cut and all-later identity before the 8B run. Audit the exact16-subset grid, labels, margins/interaction arithmetic, control bounds and registered verdict independently before interpretation.
