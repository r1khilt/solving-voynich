## Question and source basis

Can learning across many independently generated processes generalize to new parameters better than repeatedly observing a small fixed set? Does a renaming-invariant representation help, and can a larger transformer or inspectable recurrence improve joint future prediction?

Prior evidence: EXP-0008 failed stronger unfamiliar-key criteria; output clusters often matched hidden clusters. EXP-0009 broad interventions did not isolate a state rule. EXP-0010 longer contexts did not pass. EXP-0011's separate supervised null-removal gate also failed; it is not a positive starting point for manuscript deletion.

Methods reviewed: recurrence encoding (D13), ALICE random-cipher learning (D14), PFN task-prior training (T12), explicit HMM/PSR inference (T03–T10), and recurrent expressivity/DeltaProduct (A11–A12/A22). Exact primary sources and assumptions are in the [deep review](../research/deep-review-2026-09-21/README.md). DeltaProduct and ALICE v2 methods were revisited live before implementation. Our signed vector recurrence is a simplified Householder-product adaptation, not a paper replication; no paper proves it recovers Voynich.

## Fixed observations and splits

Authoritative config: [exp0012.json](../../configs/exp0012.json). Alphabet four; no external text or manuscript input. Four training families: cycle, branch, pair parity, IID. Two excluded-family challenges: third-bit XOR and switching regimes. Every task samples continuous parameters plus an alphabet permutation. “New task/key” means new process parameters, not a claim that four-symbol permutations are inexhaustible.

Train data seed 12120000. Fixed regime: 32 task keys, offsets 1000..1031. Fresh regime: **153,600 different parameter seeds**, offsets 100000..253599, one task per episode. Both regimes contain 153,600 streams of 129 symbols. Streams are not reset mid-episode and all conditions see exactly the same visible arrays within their regime.

Development: four tasks per training family, eight streams each, parameter offsets 20000000 + family_index*1000 + task_index. Confirmation: eight tasks per six families, eight streams each, offsets 30000000 + family_index*1000 + task_index. The data module records task identity and parameter hashes; preparation saves array/file checksums before fitting. Confirmation oracles and task descriptions are read only in the evaluate stage after all checkpoints are selected and frozen.

The canonical representation orders glyphs by first occurrence using prefix information only. An unseen next glyph is NEW; its event probability is divided uniformly among unobserved members of the known alphabet. Scoring is always over the original four raw symbols. This preserves bijective-renaming equivariance, not homophone/channel invariance. Suffix-invariance and all-permutation tests are implementation checks, not scientific transfer results.

Raw/canonical wrappers share parameter counts, including an unused output entry in the raw transformer. The large model is a capacity comparison with matched target exposure, **not matched compute**. Recurrent comparisons are neither parameter nor compute matched; report those limits.

Ignore first32 next-symbol targets per stream for training and main scoring. All receive 14,745,600 scored targets; total 309,657,600 across21 runs includes repeated exposure across conditions/seeds. The fresh pool provides 19,814,400 generated symbol observations; these are simulations, not additional historical evidence.

Development evaluation every400 steps; select minimum development loss among those three endpoints. ## Measurements and decision rules

Primary: raw-symbol bits after warmup, aggregated within each independently sampled task and then across keys. Compare:
fresh–fixed for raw and canonical; canonical–raw under fresh tasks; large–small canonical; GRU–raw transformer; signed–GRU. Report each family, pooled familiar families and pooled excluded families separately.

A registered predictive gain needs mean difference < −0.02 bits and task-bootstrap upper95% <0 after averaging the three model seeds per task. These are descriptive paired bootstrap intervals with 2,000 resamples, not a multiplicity-corrected global discovery claim. All contrasts and negative results are retained; do not choose the most favorable subgroup as the primary result.

Joint metrics: exact lexicographic probabilities for all64 three-symbol continuations at two fixed 64-symbol prefixes per confirmation task. Compare with the known generator distribution: full joint KL, first-symbol KL and their difference (future dependence beyond the first symbol), plus normalization error. First-symbol gain alone is not rule recovery. Two prefixes per task limit precision. Longer-horizon/length generalization is explicitly not established by this run.

Baselines: online smoothed order0/1/2 predictors and exact oracle sequence probabilities; oracle is diagnostic only. Task families have known observational-equivalence caveats, including a deliberately nonminimal XOR description. No recovered neural coordinate or HMM state count is scored as unique hidden truth.

Renaming diagnostic: at those same prefixes, apply three fixed permutations (cyclic shift, reversal, swap0/1), invert output labels and measure immediate-distribution total variation. Canonical invariance is expected by construction; the raw model's measured sensitivity is a separate diagnostic, not new-key success by itself.

## Explicit comparator

For the first two confirmation tasks in each of six families, fit edge-emitting HMM candidates K=1,2,4,8 with two restarts and25 EM iterations. Training uses32 independently generated visible sequences; selection uses8 further independent sequences and dev NLL plus fixed complexity penalty .5*d*log(Ntrain)/Ntrain. No refit on confirmation. Parameter count d=K*(4K−1)+K−1. This penalized candidate selection can prefer too-small models; report fit histories and every candidate.

Score the **same confirmation streams, warmup and joint prefixes** as the neural subset. The explicit model receives5,120 extra visible adaptation symbols for that key; frozen neural prediction uses its prefix. It is an adaptation-budget comparison, not a fair same-information architecture ranking. Source process tensors supply sampled data/oracle diagnostics only; fitting receives arrays without latent labels.
