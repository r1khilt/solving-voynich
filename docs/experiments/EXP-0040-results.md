# EXP-0040 result — published stock/flow sampler misses this held-out edge constraint

**Audited adaptive exploratory result.** The pinned [De Stefano *Generative Frontier*](https://github.com/cadeos/voynich-frontier) `stock_flow` sampler generates a manuscript-like abundance of singleton word *types* on our held-out-size panel, but its train-to-validation terminal-glyph predictive contrast is much smaller than the real manuscript's under the [frozen EXP-0040 assay](EXP-0040.md). A simpler `stroke` variant produces more edge dependence but under-produces singleton types and has negative absolute predictive gain under the manuscript-trained boundary model. None of five frozen nonsemantic families simultaneously matches these tested properties. This falsifies **specific frozen source-trained samplers under one representation and one exposed leaf split**; it is not evidence that the manuscript has meaning, that the published paper's different metrics are wrong, or that nonsemantic generation is impossible.

Its singleton share is close to real, and its novelty gap of +.05019 **barely** crosses the separate .05 descriptive flag; that flag should not be treated as a robust discontinuity. `stroke` narrowly avoids the registered edge falsification gate because all its seed contrasts lie between +.10340 and +.14855; this does **not** mean it matches the real +.19717 point estimate. The manuscript's last-minus-first feature advantage is +.11933 bits/pair in EXP-0039; generated means are −.03756 for `stock_flow` and +.07814 for `stroke`, another descriptive constraint. Iid and grille negative controls yield approximately zero order-sensitive edge contrast, as they should.

**What this changes.** The edge pattern is a useful *discriminator* against these particular independent/surface-trained samplers: `stock_flow` achieves one lexical-tail property while missing the stronger held-out order constraint, and `stroke` has the reverse tradeoff. Yet this is an adaptive test on a repeatedly exposed nine-leaf validation split with a specific ZL basic-compound representation. The external paper used different transcription material, its own statistics and pre-tuned defaults; our negative result does not directly contradict those source-reported numbers. Generator seed variation is not the same uncertainty unit as manuscript leaves, and the conservative band is a model-criticism rule, not a formal model posterior. All candidate families may be improved; richer language/cipher processes have not been run through this panel. No historical process or plaintext is identified. A next discriminating study should strengthen the no-copy contextual predictor and compare explicit **language, cipher, and nonsemantic** processes jointly on fresh material or independent anchors, rather than optimize one statistic on these exposed leaves.
